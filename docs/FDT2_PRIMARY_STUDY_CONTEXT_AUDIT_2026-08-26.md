# FDT2 primary-study context audit for Azami-series Chapter 2 (2026-08-26)

## Disposition

The 49 rows in `data/evidence/fdt1_broad_functional_calibration_seed_v1.csv` collapse to **23 unique primary studies and 23 independent study contexts**. Repeated endpoints, treatments, populations, years, genotypes and taxa inside one article remain one research cluster; they are not additional independent contexts.

The current evidence does **not** license an FDT2 geographic meta-regression. It also does **not** license a pooled experimental-exposure moderator analysis. It does license this readiness registry and a qualitative map of imposed exposures. The reasons are structural:

- geographic coverage is sparse within each homologous trait-function estimand, and exact field coordinates/elevation are often absent, inaccessible, or printed in a nonstandard coordinate form;
- multiple populations in one paper are within-study contexts, not independent studies;
- source-population geography cannot be substituted for the environment in a greenhouse, chamber or common-garden outcome;
- temperature, UV, light, rain and water treatments differ in tissue, duration, endpoint and estimand, and several are confounded with a single chamber, greenhouse, bench or plot-level treatment;
- author affiliation is never used as study locality, and no locality is geocoded or inferred.

Accordingly, the FDT2 state is **`READINESS_REGISTRY_ONLY / STOP_BEFORE_MODERATOR_MODEL`**. Chapter 2 can retain the experimentally imposed context as mechanism calibration, but the evolutionary geographic/time analysis must obtain its environmental history from focal *Cirsium* occurrence, niche and dated-phylogeny data rather than backfilling geography from these calibration studies.

## Audit rules and missingness notation

- `seed rows -> contexts` records how many FDT1 rows came from the source and confirms that they collapse to one independent context.
- **Field geography** means a locality, coordinate or elevation explicitly printed in the primary article or its primary supplement. A named region is retained verbatim but is not geocoded.
- **Imposed exposure** means an experimental treatment such as orientation, rain, methanol wash, temperature, light, UV, watering, density or herbivore exclusion. It is not converted into a field climatic value.
- `unresolved` means the item was not reported in, or could not be recovered from, the bounded lawful primary copy inspected. It is not zero and is not inferred from affiliation, taxon range or a later paper.
- Nonstandard coordinates with minutes greater than 59 are transcribed verbatim and marked unusable pending author/source correction.

## Moderator readiness by context type

| proposed FDT2 context | current status | defensible use now | reason it is not a fitted moderator now |
|---|---|---|---|
| field latitude/longitude/elevation | **STOP** | retain exact printed values and coordinate-system metadata in a registry | only a subset has usable exact geography; systems and estimands differ; some coordinates are invalid as printed; multiple sites within a paper are one cluster |
| named region/site | **registry only** | provenance and study-setting description | cannot be converted to coordinates or climate without a separately authorized georeferencing protocol |
| population/site within one study | **within-study context only** | preserve tested interaction or heterogeneity in *Aquilegia*, *Bejaria* and *Pedicularis* | populations are not independent papers and cannot inflate the study-level denominator |
| field year/season | **within-study context only** | retain when repeated years were explicitly modelled | few homologous estimates and often no year-specific effect/covariance |
| temperature | **qualitative mechanism map; pooled model STOP** | preserve exact imposed °C, duration and acute/chronic class | only a few pigment-pathway studies; tissue/endpoints differ; single chamber/greenhouse per temperature is common |
| UV/light | **qualitative mechanism map; pooled model STOP** | preserve wavelength/filter/transmission/irradiance and duration where explicit | HBA tissue filters, whole plants and common gardens are not homologous; single bench/subchamber confounding occurs |
| rain/water/drought | **qualitative mechanism map; pooled model STOP** | distinguish natural rain, artificial wetting, drainage and watering interval | exposure metrics and endpoints are incommensurate; exact dose frequently unresolved |
| biotic treatment/density | **within-study context only** | preserve herbivory, stickiness removal, bract/spine removal, density and visitor guild | mechanisms and outcome scales differ across modules; not a common geographic moderator |
| setting class | **registry only** | stratify provenance as wild field, common garden, greenhouse/chamber, lab/tissue or mixed | setting is confounded with trait module, taxon, exposure and endpoint in this 23-study seed |

## Minimum record required before reopening FDT2

Any later moderator analysis should be built from an effect-level table with, at minimum:

1. `source_id`, `study_cluster_id`, `population_id`, `site_id`, `year_id`, `block_id`, `plant_id` and the actual analysis-level biological unit;
2. explicit separation of `field_latitude/longitude/elevation` from `experimental_exposure_type/value/unit/duration`;
3. a homologous trait-function-outcome estimand, its sampling variance and covariance for shared controls or repeated plants;
4. a prespecified independence rule that keeps all rows from one source inside one study cluster; and
5. sufficient independent studies within the same module/estimand to estimate a moderator without using population, endpoint or tube/flower rows as pseudo-studies.

This audit does not set an opportunistic numeric minimum after seeing outcomes. The threshold and moderator families must be preregistered before effect access or modelling.

## Bounded STOP and Chapter 2 hand-off

The bounded primary-source audit stops here. Unresolved fields above remain unresolved; no additional locality search, affiliation geocoding, taxon-range inference, climate extraction or secondary-source substitution is authorized by this registry.

For the Azami-series Chapter 2 sequence:

- FDT1 may continue to use these 23 studies for trait-to-function calibration under their existing claim ceilings.
- FDT2 is limited to this context/readiness registry; no geographic or pooled exposure meta-regression is run.
- FDT3–FDT7 must treat rows from the same source as one study cluster and propagate setting/exposure uncertainty rather than assign missing values.
- The next geographic/time evidence must come from the focal *Cirsium* dated tree, tip-level trait provenance, historical occurrence/niche records and explicit environmental layers. These calibration-study localities are not substitutes for the geographic history of the East Asian radiation.
