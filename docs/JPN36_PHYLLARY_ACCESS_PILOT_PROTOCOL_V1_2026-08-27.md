# JPN36 non-destructive phyllary-access pilot protocol v1

Status: protocol-ready; field execution not authorized (2026-08-27)

Canonical machine-readable contract:

`data/evidence/jpn36_phyllary_access_pilot_contract_v1.json`

## 1. Current evidence lock

The focal unit is JPN36, *Cirsium sieboldii*. The authority-matched state is appressed phyllaries and nonsticky/nearly-nonsticky involucres. In the frozen Japan38 history, a terminal phyllary change at JPN36 is forced in 0.754 of 1,000 UFBoot trees.

This makes JPN36 a transition-localized target, not an adaptation result. The functional evidence for protective bracts is analogical and cannot be transported as a demonstrated *Cirsium* mechanism.

FDT8 ranks JPN36 first for field execution because a non-destructive pilot is more feasible than beginning with the conservation-sensitive JPN06/JPN15 stickiness system. That ranking does not grant access, manipulation or collection permission at any site.

## 2. Ordered gates

1. Confirm identity, live appressed phyllaries and the natural nonsticky/nearly-nonsticky state without manipulation.
2. Record an observation-only same-population reference of at least 30 heads from 30 plants. Freeze an access-gap target inside the observed 10th-90th percentile range before allocation.
3. Qualify one frozen active/sham device on authorized nonexperimental or cultivated material for 24 hours. Any device or threshold change creates a new protocol version.
4. Obtain site-matched land-manager, manipulation, conservation, terminal collection and viability-assay approval records. Complete a flowering-individual census.
5. Only after all gates pass, randomize 12 two-individual pairs (24 plants; one experimental head per plant) 1:1 to access proxy or sham.

The randomized pilot is blocked if 24 heads exceed either the authority quota or 2% of counted flowering individuals. It is not run underfilled and then interpreted as an efficacy test.

## 3. Operation and sham

### Active access proxy

Fit a removable inert external collar/frame around the involucre. Blunt external spacer tabs support selected outer phyllaries to the frozen access-gap target. The device must not cut, puncture, glue, coat or remove phyllaries, contact florets/ovaries/styles, or intentionally reorient the capitulum.

### Sham

Fit the same collar/frame with matched material, mass, visible profile, attachment positions and handling duration. Spacer tabs remain flush and do not displace phyllaries.

This contrast estimates the effect of the qualified external access perturbation. It is not equivalent to removing phyllaries or recreating a historical natural state.

## 4. Device qualification

Use at least six heads per arm for 24 hours. All of the following are required:

- no tissue tear, puncture, detached phyllary, abnormal exudation or wilt;
- no contact with florets, ovaries or styles;
- all devices remain attached and are removed intact;
- every active head reaches the frozen target range;
- median sham gap change is less than 25% of median active gap change;
- unintended head-orientation change is at most 5 degrees in every head.

Failure closes v1. The device is not retuned after visitor, enemy or seed outcomes have been viewed.

## 5. Eligibility, matching and allocation

The individual plant is the biological unit. Repeated visitor/enemy bouts are repeated observations, not independent replicates.

Before assignment, pairs must match on site, population, date, phenological stage, capitulum-diameter bin and baseline-damage bin. Individual and capitulum IDs must be unique; one head is used per plant. Rights/conservation record IDs and the device-qualification record must match the supplied deidentified authorization record.

`analysis/randomize_jpn36_phyllary_access_pairs_v1.py` uses the frozen seed `JPN36-PHYLLARY-ACCESS-V1-20260827` and SHA256 of `seed|pair_id` to assign one member of every pair to `access_proxy` and the other to `sham`. No replacement is allowed after assignment. Mechanical failure, loss and missing outcomes remain in the intention-to-treat integrity ledger.

## 6. Required observations

### Baseline and integrity

- identity and natural state;
- phenology, diameter, access gap, phyllary angle and head orientation;
- baseline enemy damage and weather context;
- device/material IDs, handling duration, target and achieved gap;
- device retention, plant contact, tissue injury and treatment integrity at every bout.

### Legitimate visitors

- observation duration;
- approaches, landings and probing visits;
- effective contacts with reproductive structures;
- visit duration.

Visitor counts are not fitness and effective contact is not itself verified pollen transfer.

### Enemies

- approaches, contact/entry attempts and successful access;
- florivory events and external damage fraction;
- predispersal seed-predator evidence.

Low-enemy periods are low-information; they do not establish absence of a defence effect.

### Reproduction

- authorized recovery of the mature head;
- total and filled achenes;
- frozen viability method and viable achenes;
- missingness and attrition reason.

Without explicit terminal collection and viability approval, the randomized full-chain pilot does not begin. Observation-only state work may continue only within its separate authorization ceiling.

## 7. Stop rules

### Before start

- stop if any approval is absent, expired, site-mismatched or narrower than the planned operation and terminal sampling;
- stop if taxon/state confirmation fails;
- stop if the census, 2% cap or authority quota cannot accommodate 24 plants;
- stop if device qualification or the frozen natural-range target is absent.

### Individual head

Remove the device on tear, puncture, detachment, abnormal exudation, wilt, reproductive-tissue contact or more than 5 degrees unintended orientation change. Keep the head under its assigned arm in the integrity/attrition ledger; do not replace it.

### Batch

Stop new assignments and remove devices if two of the first four assigned heads have any device-attributable injury/contact event. Stop immediately if an authority withdraws approval, a protected non-target organism is affected, weather creates an unreviewed device hazard, or active treatment is not measurably separable from sham without retuning.

### Analysis

- no per-protocol replacement of the intention-to-treat safety/integrity report;
- no null/equivalence claim from a nonsignificant pilot contrast;
- no new mediator, window or device threshold after outcome inspection;
- no adaptation claim from a technical-feasibility pass.

## 8. Decision and claim boundary

Technical pass requires all authorization and device gates, preserved balanced allocation, no batch stop, complete integrity/attrition accounting, and an active access perturbation distinct from sham but inside the frozen natural reference range.

Visitor, enemy and achene contrasts are exploratory pilot estimates with uncertainty. A separately powered, preregistered successor is required for efficacy or equivalence.

This protocol can show only whether one qualified external access proxy was safely deployable and auditable at an authorized site. It cannot show that natural appressed phyllaries are adaptive, caused the historical transition, are homologous to other protective bracts, generally exclude enemies, improve pollination or increase fitness across *Cirsium*.
