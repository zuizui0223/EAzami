# *Cirsium* phylogeny literature screening — batch 03

Date: 2026-08-10

## Why this batch was needed

The previous evidence map covered the principal 2002–2026 molecular and regional studies but still left two temporal gaps:

1. the foundational 1999–2000 Carduinae ITS/morphological framework;
2. the most recent 2025–2026 phylogenomic and tribe-wide plastome studies.

This batch closes those gaps without treating old few-locus or organelle trees as equivalent to modern nuclear species trees.

## Curated records

### Häffner & Hellwig 1999 — Tier B

**DOI:** `10.3372/wi.29.2902`

- broad ITS analysis of Cardueae with emphasis on Carduinae;
- tested generic affinities and the relation between Carduinae and Centaureinae;
- public electronic supplement preserves the sequence matrix and coded gaps.

**Use:** historical molecular topology and data provenance.  
**Limit:** one linked multicopy nuclear-ribosomal region cannot resolve rapid radiation, gene flow or homeolog histories.

### Häffner 2000 — Tier C

**DOI:** `10.2307/3776757`

- 208-page morphological/anatomical cladistic monograph;
- 52 phylogenetically informative characters encoded as 66 transitions;
- 37 ingroup and three outgroup genus-level taxa;
- recognized a Carduus–*Cirsium* group and other major Carduinae complexes.

**Use:** preserves the morphological hypotheses and character systems that later molecular analyses tested.  
**Limit:** genus-level morphology produced 48 most-parsimonious trees and is not sequence evidence.

### Moreyra et al. 2025 — Tier A

**DOI:** `10.1111/jbi.70045`

- 489 nuclear loci;
- comprehensive Afromontane/Afroalpine *Afrocarduus* sampling;
- ancestral-range and diversification analysis;
- inferred a late-Miocene Afromontane origin and early-Pleistocene diversification.

**Use:** current comparative mountain-radiation study within the Carduus–*Cirsium* group using a compatible Hyb-Seq framework.

### Moreyra et al. 2026 — Tier A

**DOI:** `10.1111/jse.70024`

- 489 nuclear loci plus morphology;
- recovered 16 evolutionary lineages and expanded accepted *Afrocarduus* diversity from eight to 18 species;
- found two independent origins of the stemless habit;
- detected paraphyly plausibly involving ILS or hybridization.

**Use:** direct current demonstration that a conspicuous thistle growth form can evolve repeatedly and that young radiations require integrative species delimitation and discordance analysis.

### Abdullah et al. 2026 — Tier B

**DOI:** `10.1186/s12870-026-08217-2`

- complete chloroplast genomes of 72 species from 51 genera and all 12 Cardueae subtribes;
- 54 species were sequenced or assembled de novo and combined with 18 published plastomes;
- supplies ML/BI trees, alignments, scripts and accession tables;
- nine subtribes were monophyletic in the plastome analysis, whereas Saussureinae and Onopordinae were polyphyletic;
- explicitly documented cytonuclear disagreement with nuclear studies.

**Use:** strongest current tribe-wide maternal-history framework.  
**Limit:** one nonrecombining plastid genome cannot replace a nuclear species tree or resolve chloroplast capture.

## Revised field history

The evidence timeline is now continuous:

1. morphological and ITS hypotheses of Carduinae (1999–2000);
2. combined nuclear/plastid Cardueae frameworks (2002–2013);
3. reusable Compositae target enrichment and family/tribe Hyb-Seq (2014–2019);
4. global and regional *Cirsium* target capture/phylotranscriptomics (2020–2026);
5. current integrative species delimitation, reticulation, cytogenetics and whole-genome resources.

## Consequence for EAzami

No new record establishes a white-to-coloured regain. Instead, the latest evidence reinforces three design decisions:

- analyse conspicuous traits across alternative nuclear histories because parallel morphology is common;
- retain the plastid tree separately because cytonuclear disagreement is empirically documented;
- use population genomics and cytotype-aware analyses to distinguish regain from standing variation, introgression and homeolog sorting.
