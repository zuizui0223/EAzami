# Global-to-East-Asia doctoral synthesis

Status: 2026-08-22

## 1. Repository boundary: Azami ends where EAzami begins

### `zuizui0223/azami`
Azami is the global observational discovery layer. Its scientific job is:

`public images -> continuous capitulum phenotypes -> within-taxon distributions -> within/among-taxon environmental structure`

The global result is intentionally non-causal. Across the nine primary image-derived endpoints, most visible variance occurs below assigned taxon means, and orientation and visible colour show the clearest environmental structure. Gross outline is less consistently structured. Auxiliary involucre/spine-like image geometry covaries with temperature seasonality, but those quantities are 2-D proxies rather than botanical defence traits.

Azami therefore does **not** claim pollinator causation, antagonism, local adaptation, evolutionary rate, parallel evolution or adaptive radiation.

### `zuizui0223/EAzami`
EAzami starts at the question Azami cannot answer:

> **Why can a very young East Asian/Japanese *Cirsium* radiation repeatedly occupy such different capitulum phenotypes, and are those phenotypes adaptive modules rather than one undifferentiated lability axis?**

EAzami owns four downstream operations:
1. quantitative literature synthesis / meta-analysis of ecological mechanisms;
2. East Asian evolutionary-history reconstruction;
3. ancestry-resolved tests of repeated or parallel state transitions;
4. focal causal experiments linking a module to interaction/protection and reproductive fitness.

The two repositories therefore form a zoom, not competing manuscripts:

`global phenotype landscape (Azami)`

`-> ecological mechanism priors from meta-analysis`

`-> East Asian rapid-radiation history (EAzami)`

`-> repeated-state / parallel-evolution tests`

`-> focal trait -> interaction/protection -> fitness experiments`

## 2. What the global layer actually establishes

Frozen Azami evidence establishes three premises relevant to the doctorate.

1. **Within-taxon variation is not a minor residual.** Across nine endpoints, 0.589-0.931 of visible image variance occurs below assigned-taxon means.
2. **Orientation and colour are the strongest global evolutionary candidates.** Both retain the clearest within- and among-taxon environmental structure after spatial modelling, whereas gross outline is weaker/model-dependent.
3. **Involucre/spine-like geometry is environmentally structured but functionally unresolved.** Within taxa, temperature seasonality is positively associated with involucre roughness (beta=0.0975), outward projection fraction (0.0937) and maximum spine-like projection (0.0911), all q=0.0424. These are image proxies only.

This global layer is a discovery map. The doctoral programme asks why these same modules can change quickly and repeatedly.

## 3. What the literature meta-analysis resolves before looking at East Asia

### 3.1 Pollinators and antagonists: no universal dominant agent

The strict dual-manipulation synthesis now contains five independent study programmes / five taxa in which pollination and antagonism are both experimentally manipulated. Outcomes are:

- antagonist-dominant: 2/5;
- pollinator-dominant: 1/5;
- mixed / no fixed dominance: 2/5.

At least two dominance categories remain after every leave-one-program-out deletion. Thus neither `pollinators generally dominate floral selection` nor `antagonists generally dominate floral selection` is defensible as a universal model.

**Working conclusion:** floral evolution is better represented as a **selection mosaic** whose locally realized net selection depends on interaction opportunity, trait-specific leverage and downstream fitness gates.

### 3.2 Pollinator dependence is not pollen limitation

The six-study *Cirsium* reproductive-assurance synthesis shows that animal pollination can contribute strongly to seed production while current open reproduction is not necessarily pollen-limited. Selfing ability, effective donor spacing, flowering overlap and local density change reproductive assurance.

Therefore `visitor abundance -> pollination benefit` is not an acceptable causal shortcut.

### 3.3 Antagonist cost is quantitatively established

The harmonized reproductive-herbivory meta-analysis contains four independent data-generation studies and nine within-study contrasts. Experimental reduction of insect herbivory increases viable/mature seed output by:

- pooled RR = **2.674**;
- 95% CI = **2.388-2.993**;
- equivalent ambient-herbivory loss of potential seed output = **62.6%**.

The doctorate no longer needs to ask whether reproductive antagonists can matter. It asks **which capitulum module changes that cost and what mutualist/abiotic cost accompanies the change**.

### 3.4 Fecundity effects pass through demographic gates

Across six *Cirsium* demographic studies / four taxa, reproductive-insect effects on fecundity transmit consistently to recruitment/population growth in four studies, are context-dependent in one and blocked in one. Explicit demographic gates are supported in 3/4 tests.

Thus environmental context often acts not as a simple monotonic climate coefficient but at the seed-to-recruitment bottleneck through safe sites, disturbance and density dependence.

### 3.5 Broad trait classes and interaction intensity are insufficient

A restricted validation meta-analysis of 38 standardized pollinator-mediated selection gradients from six independent articles / six taxa did not identify a universal hierarchy among phenology, plant display, flower sensory/display and pollination-efficiency classes. In the *Lobelia* manipulation, stronger pollen limitation did not strengthen selection on every trait.

**Refined general mechanism:**

`realized biotic selection = interaction opportunity x local trait-to-interaction leverage x downstream fitness gating`

This equation is conceptual, not a fitted multiplicative coefficient.

## 4. Zoom to East Asia: why this is a rapid-radiation problem

Moreyra et al. (2025; DOI 10.1016/j.ympev.2025.108285) recovered a Pleistocene jump dispersal from Asia to Japan at approximately 2.4 Ma (95% interval 1.7-3.6 Ma), followed by rapid diversification. Their sampling included 38 Japanese species; all but two belonged to the dominant radiation. The paper explicitly proposes an interplay of allopatric and ecological speciation rather than one single driver.

Chang et al. (2026; DOI 10.1186/s12870-026-08097-6) further resolve shallow East Asian lineages and show that floral-colour categories need not match evolutionary lineages. In the Taiwanese *C. japonicum* complex, white and bluish-purple forms can occur within the same named lineage, and the authors identify introgression, parallel mutation and ecological selection as alternatives requiring denser work.

These results change the doctoral question. The remarkable feature is not merely that different *Cirsium* species have different heads. It is that **substantial capitulum disparity accumulated inside a shallow, reticulation-prone Pleistocene radiation**.

The correct claim at present is **rapid radiation with a candidate adaptive component**. `Adaptive radiation` becomes justified only when a focal capitulum state is linked through an ecological mechanism to reproductive fitness.

## 5. Four focal capitulum modules

### 5.1 Colour: loss, regain and regulatory reuse

#### Literature hypothesis
Flower-colour transitions are not universally pollinator-driven and can be asymmetric (Rausher 2008; DOI 10.1086/523358). Molecular work in other flowering-plant radiations shows two competing evolutionary routes:

1. **reversible regulatory suppression:** core anthocyanin genes remain intact after floral pigment loss, preserving the capacity for regain (e.g. Ho & Smith 2016; DOI 10.1186/s12862-016-0675-3; Iochrominae includes a documented regain);
2. **structural/developmental constraint:** loss-of-function or pathway-branch inactivation can make some transitions less readily reversible.

Convergent flower-colour shifts frequently involve repeated changes in pathway expression rather than complete reconstruction of pigment biosynthesis.

#### What EAzami already resolves
East Asian tip-state data show recurrent white/coloured polymorphism or close juxtaposition, but they do **not** establish transition direction. In the Arenicola contrast, equal-cost parsimony and low-rate Mk scenarios favour a coloured ancestor and a white loss in *C. brevicaule*, but the transition rate is not identified.

Primary dated sensitivity:
- q=0.2/Myr -> P(coloured Arenicola MRCA)=0.919;
- q=0.5 -> 0.773;
- q=1 -> 0.604;
- high q -> ~0.5.

Expanding to ten fixed-colour East Asian tips and 36 topology/nuisance scenarios strengthens the **conditional** low-rate coloured-ancestor signal (P(C)=0.955-0.966 at q=0.2), but every LR95 profile still reaches 100 transitions/Myr and the supported ancestral-state range still reaches 0.5.

#### New doctoral hypothesis
> White states may often be regulatory suppression of a retained anthocyanin module; if so, coloured expression can reappear repeatedly when the ecological selection mosaic changes.

But `C. irumtiense is a colour regain` remains an explicit competing hypothesis, **not a recovered conclusion**.

#### What is still required
- accepted common-locus branch-length nuclear topology;
- population-aware W/C states and independent transition reconstruction;
- at least two independent W/C transitions with the same-individual chain:
  `ancestry -> coding/regulatory haplotype -> floral RNA -> pigment -> calibrated colour`;
- Aim 2 fitness tests where flowering overlap permits.

Parallel ecological colour evolution and molecular reuse must be tested separately.

### 5.2 Orientation: repeated erect/nodding states and protection

#### Literature hypothesis
Nodding orientation need not evolve because pollinators simply prefer nodding flowers. In *Cremanthodium campanulatum* (Asteraceae; DOI 10.1080/17550874.2012.702793), experimentally erect heads had much lower achene set than natural nodding heads, while pollinator preference did not differ. Water and UV-B reduced pollen viability, supporting protection from wetting/UV as a causal route.

#### What EAzami already resolves
The current Japanese image-authority overlap is directionally consistent with botanical erect/nodding categories, but contains only eight taxa and is not significant (two-sided exact permutation p=0.314). It validates neither gravity orientation nor ancestral states.

The mechanism-reduction screen rejects a static `orientation -> pollinator preference` story as sufficient. The focal preregistration therefore distinguishes:
- ORI1: time-window / thermal-pollination pathway;
- ORI2: wetting / pollen-protection pathway;
- ORI3: antagonist-exposure pathway;
- ORI4: combined interaction partitioning;
- ORI0: null-compatible only after equivalence bounds;
- ORI5: fitness effect with an unexplained pathway.

#### New doctoral hypothesis
> Erect/nodding states may have arisen repeatedly in East Asian *Cirsium* because orientation repeatedly solves the same time-window/protection problem under different local environments, even if the underlying mutations are not identical.

#### What is still required
- botanical orientation states for East Asian/Japanese populations mapped onto an accepted nuclear branch-length tree;
- reconstruction of independent transitions before using the words `parallel` or `convergent`;
- focal sham/reorientation experiments linking process shifts to filled achenes.

### 5.3 Phyllary spines and stickiness: separate defence hypotheses

These traits must not be merged into one generic `defence` score.

#### Spines/phyllaries
The general Cardueae/Asteraceae literature supports the plausibility that spiny involucral architecture can exclude damaging visitors and can impose handling/access costs on legitimate floral visitors. Azami additionally finds seasonality-associated spine-like image geometry. However, there is no current focal *Cirsium* experiment demonstrating that the measured phyllary/spine state preserves seed output by reducing antagonism.

**Hypothesis:**
`spine/phyllary architecture -> antagonist access/damage down -> seed fitness up`, potentially accompanied by `pollinator effective contact down`.

This remains conditional until the image proxy is replaced by direct botanical measurements and a defensible manipulation.

#### Stickiness
The direct *C. discolor* neutralization experiment (Thomas 2003; DOI 10.22543/0090-0222.2085) found no increase in seed predators and no decrease in seed production when sticky traps were neutralized. Earlier site/year observations are not sufficient to rescue a universal defence claim.

**Conclusion:** generic `stickiness = reproductive defence` is weakened and should remain a low-priority / negative-control lane unless focal data give a reason to reopen it.

### 5.4 Capitulum size and head number: display is a benefit-cost curve, not `bigger is better`

*Cirsium purpuratum* provides the strongest focal literature bridge.

Pollinator side:
- visitation per plant rises deceleratingly with floral display;
- heads probed per plant increase with display but less than proportionally;
- local plant density changes these responses (Ohashi & Yahara 1998, DOI 10.2307/2446309; Ohashi & Yahara 2002, DOI 10.1046/j.1365-2435.2002.00644.x).

Antagonist side:
- in two *C. purpuratum* populations, the proportion of seeds/heads preyed upon increased with flower production, while mature-seed proportion did not increase with flower number (Ohashi & Yahara 2000; DOI 10.1139/b99-182).

Broader Asteraceae size-predation comparisons point in the same direction, but use different estimands and cannot be pooled with the *C. purpuratum* behavioural slopes.

**Working hypothesis:**
> Selection on capitulum size / head number is density- and context-dependent because additional display changes both mutualist discovery/probing and antagonist discovery. The optimum is therefore a local benefit-cost surface rather than monotonically larger display.

**Required focal data:**
`display + local density -> visits / heads probed / effective contacts + antagonist exposure -> filled achenes`.

## 6. The integrated evolutionary hypothesis generated by the synthesis

The literature and existing analyses now support a more precise doctoral model than `environment causes trait divergence`:

`environment + spatial/population context`

`-> interaction opportunity / reproductive assurance / enemy pressure`

`-> local functional leverage of colour, orientation, display or defence architecture`

`-> pollination / protection / antagonism`

`-> seed fitness`

`-> recruitment gate`

Different capitulum modules may enter at different arrows. If ancestry-resolved data show that these paths vary semi-independently and are repeatedly re-used across shallow lineages, that is the empirical content of **modular evolvability**.

This generates a specific explanation for rapid East Asian radiation:

> **Rapid diversification may be accelerated when a young, reticulating radiation carries reusable phenotypic/developmental modules that can be reassigned to different local selection mosaics without waiting for deep genome-wide lineage sorting.**

This is the thesis hypothesis, not yet the thesis conclusion.

## 7. What is solved by existing data versus what requires the doctorate

### Already resolved / working at the meta or observational level
- global below-taxon capitulum variation is large;
- global environmental structure is strongest for orientation and visible colour;
- reproductive antagonist cost in *Cirsium* is large and repeatable;
- pollinator dependence and pollen limitation are distinct;
- demographic transmission is gated;
- fixed pollinator dominance and fixed antagonist dominance are both poor general models;
- display produces simultaneous mutualist and antagonist responses;
- generic stickiness defence is weakened;
- the main Japanese radiation is young enough to make repeated-module evolution a meaningful hypothesis.

### Existing EAzami analyses can still resolve without new field biology
- accepted-tree East Asian mapping of colour and botanical orientation once compatible branch-length data are secured;
- number/direction of independent colour/orientation transitions;
- whether environmental contexts repeat across independent transitions;
- whether common-lability or module-specific models better predict the compiled observational pattern once transition identities are trustworthy.

### Cannot be resolved by more generic literature or present observational data
- whether *C. irumtiense* specifically represents coloured re-expression after white loss;
- whether repeated erect/nodding states are truly independent parallel transitions;
- whether Japanese *Cirsium* phyllary spines are defensive adaptations;
- whether local display optima are caused by the predicted pollinator-antagonist balance;
- which agent dominates selection in each focal population;
- whether the same genomic/developmental module underlies repeated phenotypic transitions;
- whether the Japanese rapid radiation is demonstrably **adaptive**.

These are the empirical doctoral gates and belong in the single active doctoral issue rather than in Azami.

## Claim discipline

`Meta-analysis supports` means a hypothesis survived the stated quantitative or falsification synthesis; it is not mathematical proof of a universal biological law. Structured syntheses are kept separate from pooled effect-size meta-analyses when estimands are biologically incompatible.
