# External cross-check: mutualist–antagonist selection mosaic

## Why HGA3 was renamed

The first EAzami formulation, `conditional_antagonist_priority`, correctly captured one local prediction from the Cirsium meta-syntheses: reproductive-antagonist costs are consistently detectable, whereas current open pollen limitation can be buffered by reproductive assurance.

However, broader experimental literature shows that **neither pollinators nor antagonists are universally the stronger agent of floral selection**. The defensible general hypothesis is therefore a context-dependent **selection mosaic**.

EAzami now uses:

`HGA3 = assurance_dependent_selection_mosaic`

> The relative marginal fitness contribution of pollinator-facing versus antagonist-protection traits switches across populations and times as pollination assurance and antagonist pressure change.

## External checks

### Spatial selection mosaic

Sletvold et al. / Primula farinosa work showed that pollinator-mediated selection favored a more exposed floral display whereas grazing favored the opposite morph, and differences in interaction strength among populations predicted spatial variation in selection and subsequent morph-frequency change.

Interpretation for EAzami: opposing agents can change rank among nearby populations; a universal `pollinator-dominant` or `antagonist-dominant` model is too strong.

### Pollinator-dominant systems exist

Experimental work in Lobelia cardinalis found stronger pollinator-mediated than herbivore-mediated selection on several floral traits.

Interpretation for EAzami: the large Cirsium antagonist seed cost cannot be converted into a universal claim that antagonists dominate floral evolution.

### Herbivore/defence-dominant systems also exist

Work in Trifolium repens found that herbivory and antiherbivore defence altered selection on reproductive traits and, in one study, herbivore-associated effects were stronger overall than pollinator-associated effects.

Interpretation for EAzami: the relative ranking of selective agents is itself an ecological response variable.

### Antagonists can alter the optimum amount of pollinator attraction

Fitch & Vandermeer (2021; DOI 10.1086/716637) formalized `antagonist-induced adaptive pollen limitation`: correlated attraction of pollinators and antagonists can make reduced investment in attraction fitness-optimal even when it leaves reproduction pollen limited.

EAzami does not assume this exact mechanism for Cirsium, but it supplies an important alternative to the idea that pollen limitation always implies selection for greater attraction.

## Relation to HGA4

`HGA4 = interaction_partitioning_tradeoff_mitigation` is not proposed as a new general ecological concept. It explicitly connects the EAzami Cirsium evidence to the established trade-off-mitigation framework of Ohashi, Jürgens & Thomson (2021; Biological Reviews, DOI 10.1111/brv.12754).

The EAzami-specific contribution is the testable application:

- orientation: time-window pollination + wetting/protection;
- scent: diel separation of pollinator and florivore exposure;
- display: simultaneous mutualist gain and antagonist exposure;
- colour: local-frequency-dependent choice rather than fixed preference.

The focal prediction is that some capitulum modules improve net fitness by changing **when, where or how efficiently** interactions occur, not necessarily by maximizing total visitor counts.

## Resulting experimental prediction

The strongest discrimination design is not a comparison of literature effect magnitudes. It is a same-population, same-fitness-scale test:

`pollination assurance × antagonist pressure × capitulum module -> filled achenes / seed mass`

with local flowering density, donor spacing, time-window effective contacts and antagonist exposure measured explicitly.

If the selection-mosaic hypothesis is correct:

- pollinator-mediated trait benefit should become stronger in low-assurance contexts;
- antagonist-mediated trait benefit should become stronger under high enemy pressure;
- the sign or magnitude of net trait selection may switch among populations without requiring a different broad climate optimum;
- a module that partitions interactions may retain high fitness across more than one interaction regime.

## Boundary

External studies validate the plausibility and framing of the hypothesis; they do not constitute evidence that the same selection mosaic operates in Cirsium. HGA3 remains `candidate_supported_not_identified` until the focal factorial test is run.
