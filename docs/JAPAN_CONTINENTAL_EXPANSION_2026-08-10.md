# Japan polymorphism systems become transregional East Asian tests

## Why the scope changes

The Japanese white-flower systems should not be treated as Japan-only evolutionary events without checking their continental populations.

### Cirsium pendulum

Verified sources show that *C. pendulum* is native from southern Siberia through Japan and China, with Korea, Manchuria, Primorye and other continental regions included in the accepted range. The National Museum of Nature and Science explicitly recognizes a white-flowered form (Shirobana-taka-azami), and Plants of the World Online lists historical white-flower names such as `f. albiflorum`, `var. albiflorum` and `var. leucanthum` among its synonyms.

Moreyra et al. (2025) mention *C. pendulum* as a broad Middle/East Asian–Japanese generalist. However, the current repository has not yet verified its exact tip placement in the Moreyra 350-locus trees. Therefore, do not assign a Japanese-radiation sister relationship until the exact tree artifact is recovered.

### Cirsium sieboldii

The National Museum of Nature and Science describes the ordinary florets as reddish purple and explicitly notes a white-flowered form, Shirobana-kiseru-azami. Its database still describes the species as Japanese endemic.

That geographic statement is superseded for this project by Jin & Chen (2022), who examined type material and living plants and synonymized the Chinese Zhejiang taxon *C. paludigenum* with *C. sieboldii*. The species therefore has a verified Chinese continental extension relevant to population history.

The exact *C. sieboldii* placement in Moreyra et al. (2025) remains unverified from the accessible main text, so it must stay `nuclear_placement_pending` rather than being grafted by taxonomy.

## New hypotheses

### JP-C1 — geographically derived white loss
Continental and Japanese coloured populations represent the ancestral coloured background, and Japanese white populations arose by one or more derived C->W losses.

Prediction: white populations are nested within coloured population ancestry and share a derived colour-suppression allele/haplotype.

### JP-C2 — repeated local white loss
White flowers evolved independently in more than one Japanese population or region.

Prediction: white populations do not share one causal haplotype, or different regulatory lesions converge on the same anthocyanin phenotype.

### JP-C3 — old standing colour polymorphism
White/coloured alleles predate the present Japan–continent population split.

Prediction: colour-associated haplotypes are deeper than geographic population structure and may be distributed across Japan and continental populations.

### JP-C4 — introgressed colour state
A colour-associated haplotype was acquired through gene flow from another lineage.

Prediction: local ancestry around the causal region conflicts with the genome-wide population tree.

## Sampling consequence

For *C. pendulum*, the minimum useful design is no longer `Japan white vs Japan purple` alone. It becomes:

1. Japanese white populations;
2. nearby Japanese coloured populations;
3. Korean / NE Chinese / Russian-Far-East coloured bridge populations, chosen after population structure and permits are assessed.

For *C. sieboldii*:

1. Japanese white populations;
2. nearby Japanese coloured populations;
3. Zhejiang populations confirmed under the Jin & Chen treatment.

## Nuclear-phylogeny caution

Moreyra et al. (2025) included 38 Japanese species and showed a major Pleistocene Japanese radiation, but the accessible main text does not enumerate all 38 tips. `C. pendulum` is mentioned as a broad-range generalist; `C. sieboldii` is not found in the accessible main text. Exact supplementary/tree recovery remains necessary before formal ancestral-state reconstruction.

## Scope-expansion rule

The project should expand concentrically rather than by political borders:

1. Japan + Ryukyu + Taiwan — focal white/polymorphic systems and strongest nuclear anchors;
2. eastern China + Korea — continental populations of focal taxa and additional colour transitions;
3. NE China + Russian Far East — range-edge bridge populations for broad taxa such as *C. pendulum*;
4. broader China / Mongolia / East Asia — only when taxa add independent white losses, regain candidates, or necessary nuclear backbone context.

Every new region enters through the same filter: flower-colour information gain first, generic taxon completion second.
