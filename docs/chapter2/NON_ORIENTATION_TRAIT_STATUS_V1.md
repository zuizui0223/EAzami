# Chapter 2 non-orientation trait status

**Status date:** 2026-09-04

This note corrects an over-simplified routing in which non-orientation traits were treated mainly as archive/future material. The current repository contains three distinct non-orientation evidence classes that must not be conflated: (1) phyllary posture history, (2) direct phyllary/spine morphology and function, and (3) flower-colour continuous history/current ecology. Stickiness is a fourth discrete historical module with a separate functional boundary.

## 1. Phyllary posture — already a core historical result

This is not an unfinished spine proxy. Authority-backed phyllary posture is one of the three completed discrete historical traits.

- resolved concepts: 10;
- minimum changes: exactly 3 across all 1,000 UFBoot topologies;
- relative-depth median envelope: 0.695–1.000;
- paired lower-bound ordering: phyllary deeper than orientation on 993/1000 topologies and deeper than stickiness on 1000/1000;
- coverage-matched central ordering persists, although strict deep tails overlap.

**Role:** primary mainline evidence for mosaic assembly and unequal evolutionary depth.

## 2. Direct phyllary/spine morphology — promising but not yet a Japan38 historical module

Direct spine traits (length, direction, density/apex geometry) are biologically different from phyllary posture and should not be silently substituted for it.

Current direct evidence:

- direct phyllary length/protrusion measurements are compiled for seven focal taxa;
- restricted seven-taxon PGLS shows smaller phyllary protrusion in D taxa, beta approximately -1.58 SD with p=0.0553–0.0562;
- this is borderline and strongly vulnerable to lineage/sample composition;
- no direct *Cirsium* phyllary/spine manipulation linked through antagonist access and pollinator access to final reproductive fitness has been recovered;
- image-derived `spine_peak_count_proxy` and `spine_relative_length_max_proxy` exist from Azami and have explicit QC, but they remain proxy geometry rather than validated botanical spine traits.

**Role:** mechanism candidate / Chapter 3 direct-validation target, not a fourth completed Chapter 2 history.

## 3. Stickiness — completed history, weak generic defence mechanism

Stickiness is a completed discrete historical module, not a spine surrogate.

Current Chapter 2 history:

- resolved concepts: 13 after the authority extension;
- minimum changes: exactly 5 across the current 1,000-UFBoot result;
- central relative depth is shallow/terminal-biased (median envelope about 0.937–0.954);
- it contributes to the 0/3 robust shared-transition-localization result.

Functional interpretation is weaker:

- a direct *Cirsium discolor* neutralization result weakens a generic `sticky = reproductive defence` rule;
- relaxed occurrence gates restore state diversity only through one sticky lineage in the frozen ecology panel, so current ecology is lineage-confounded.

**Role:** primary historical module plus useful negative-control for simple defence stories; not evidence for a universal defensive adaptation.

## 4. Flower colour — real signal, but current Japan38 provenance blocks promotion

Colour has more evidence than a simple side line, but the strongest continuous historical result is currently a **global exact-concept species proxy**, not a population-matched Japanese-radiation phenotype.

### Continuous history already executed

- exact image-derived continuous colour metrics exist for 14/38 Japan38 taxon concepts;
- high-depth signal uses six concepts at n>=5 and five at n>=10;
- LAB lightness is anti-phylogenetic / overdispersed in the global exact-concept proxy:
  - n>=5: rho=-0.7071, exact two-sided p=0.04444;
  - n>=10: rho=-0.8545, exact two-sided p=0.00833;
- chroma and circular hue do not show the same stable pattern.

### Current-climate explanation is not recovered

Current BIO1/BIO4/BIO12/BIO15 climatic distance does not explain the lightness overdispersion in the global exact-concept proxy.

### Geographic provenance blocks the Japan38 claim

When colour is restricted to Japan-window observations where available, the anti-phylogenetic signal weakens and is not supported:

- Japan-where-available six-concept sensitivity: rho=-0.4036, two-sided p=0.3208;
- Japan-window four-concept signal: rho=-0.3143, p=0.625;
- *C. kamtschaticum* illustrates the issue: global median lightness 65.0 vs Japan-window median 73.43.

Therefore global species medians cannot be treated as phenotypes of the Japanese nuclear-tip populations.

### Independent current RSDS sister-system result

The Azami direction `higher RSDS -> lower chroma` is reproduced in the Arenicola *C. brevicaule* (white) / *C. irumtiense* (coloured) pair, but reverses in the Taiwan *C. kawakamii* / *C. tatakaense* pair. The reversal survives 0.05-degree locality aggregation.

This is useful positive evidence for **lineage-dependent present colour ecology** and against one universal persistent RSDS driver, but it is not a historical colour-transition cause.

**Role now:** secondary cross-trait evidence that current ecological organization can be lineage-specific and need not identify origin. Do not make colour a fourth Japan38 historical module until Japan-local population-matched colour coverage passes the provenance gate. Do not invent a C/W threshold merely to force colour into the discrete Sankoff framework.

## Recommended manuscript routing

### Main biological headline

Keep the three completed discrete historical modules:

1. orientation;
2. phyllary posture;
3. stickiness.

### Main/Discussion secondary cross-trait evidence

Colour deserves a short explicit paragraph because it independently shows a similar conceptual boundary: strong-looking species-level/current structure can change with lineage and geographic provenance. It should support the paper-wide inference about scale/history dependence, not compete with the validated orientation H1–H4 chain.

### SI / direct-validation track

- full colour continuous-history and geographic-provenance analyses;
- two sister-system RSDS contrasts;
- direct phyllary protrusion seven-taxon pilot;
- Azami image-derived spine proxies.

### Future causal experiment

Directly measure, on ancestry-matched populations:

- phyllary spine length, direction and density;
- antagonist access/damage;
- pollinator effective contact;
- final filled-achene output.

This is the required chain before `spines = defence` can be promoted.
