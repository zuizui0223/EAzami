# Chapter 2 exploratory trait–environment reframe v1

Status date: 2026-09-01  
Status: **ACTIVE SCIENTIFIC REFRAME — V5 production package retained as pre-reframe audit snapshot**

## Why the reframe is necessary

The merged JEB V5 package correctly preserves the repeated-history, chronology, palaeoclimate and sister-system results, but its narrative gives too much causal priority to the two strongest Azami Chapter 1 among-taxon candidates:

- orientation × BIO12;
- corolla chroma × RSDS.

Those relationships are useful cross-chapter comparisons, but they should not define the Chapter 2 predictor universe. Azami Chapter 1 did not begin from those two rows: it began from a complete exploratory atlas of all registered continuous endpoints against nine frozen environmental predictors, then applied multiplicity, spatial and historical-placement sensitivities. The two headline candidates emerged only at the end of that process.

Chapter 2 should follow the same epistemic order while remaining an evolutionary-history chapter.

## Revised Chapter 2 question

> **Across the focal Cirsium radiation, which capitulum traits align with which present environmental axes when the trait × environment field is explored without an Azami-derived selection rule; how deep and recurrent are those traits in evolutionary history; and only then, which EAzami patterns agree with, disagree with, or are absent from the Azami global spatial atlas?**

This makes Azami an external replication/comparison layer rather than the prior that defines what EAzami is allowed to discover.

## Revised evidence order

```text
EAzami current exploratory trait × environment atlas
        ↓
trait-specific phylogenetic/topology sensitivity
        ↓
repeated-history burden + relative/datable depth
        ↓
range-history / dated-event environmental trajectories where evaluable
        ↓
Azami ↔ EAzami concordance / discordance / non-comparability
        ↓
public-data identifiability ceiling
        ↓
Chapter 3 mechanism + reproductive fitness
```

The historical layers remain the defining depth contribution of Chapter 2. The new current atlas is the ecological baseline for the focal radiation, not a second global Chapter 1.

## E1 — EAzami-only exploratory current atlas

### Trait universe

Current frozen continuous core:

1. orientation angle;
2. corolla Lab lightness;
3. corolla Lab chroma;
4. shape aspect ratio;
5. shape circularity;
6. shape solidity;
7. shape width CV.

Expansion rule: add every Azami-compatible continuous endpoint that can be measured reproducibly on the EAzami focal public-image cohort. Endpoints that fail coverage stay explicit `not_evaluable` or `unexecuted`; they are not silently removed.

Authority-coded orientation, phyllary posture and involucre stickiness remain a separate discrete state lane because their ontology differs from image-continuous traits.

### Environment universe

The final current atlas should use the same six environmental blocks as Chapter 1, but because they are a common measurement universe rather than because Chapter 1 produced significant effects:

- thermal: BIO1, BIO4;
- hydric: BIO12, BIO15;
- radiative / atmospheric: RSDS, VPD;
- mechanical: sfcWind;
- growing-season water input: GSP;
- resource / productivity: NPP.

Phase 1 already has BIO1/BIO4/BIO12/BIO15. Phase 2 expands to all nine predictors.

### Multiplicity and reporting

- test every available endpoint × predictor row;
- BH correction across the complete successful family;
- retain raw P and global-family q;
- retain coverage and leave-one-taxon-out direction;
- do not remove unsupported rows;
- raw P < 0.05 without BH support is an exploratory lead only;
- no row enters because Azami found the same pair.

## Phase-1 pilot already recovered

Using the frozen species-median snapshot and requiring at least ten balanced environmental observations per taxon gives eight taxa and 7 × 4 = 28 endpoint × climate tests. Exact taxon-label permutations enumerate all 8! = 40,320 assignments per row.

### Result

No row survives BH correction across the 28-test family.

The strongest raw exploratory lead is:

- **shape width CV × BIO12:** rho = +0.8571, exact two-sided P = 0.0107, BH q = 0.30;
- leave-one-taxon-out rho = +0.786 to +0.929, positive in every deletion.

A second raw boundary lead is:

- **corolla Lab lightness × BIO4:** rho = -0.7229, exact two-sided P = 0.0500, BH q = 0.5375;
- leave-one-taxon-out rho = -0.873 to -0.582, negative in every deletion.

Other notable but unsupported stable directions include:

- orientation angle × BIO4: rho = +0.7143, P = 0.0576, q = 0.5375, positive in every deletion;
- orientation angle × BIO15: rho = -0.4524, P = 0.2675, negative in every deletion;
- chroma × BIO1: rho = +0.3810, positive in every deletion;
- chroma × BIO15: rho = -0.3571, negative in every deletion.

Crucially, in this small EAzami-only continuous-trait panel:

- **orientation angle × BIO12 is approximately zero:** rho = +0.0238, P = 0.9768, with mixed leave-one-out signs.

This does not contradict the Azami global among-taxon BIO12 result because the taxa, scale, trait sampling and geographic scope differ. It demonstrates why the Azami candidate cannot be the Chapter 2 entry rule.

## E2 — Phylogenetic and topology gate

The final current EAzami atlas should then ask which exploratory rows remain directionally stable after accounting for the admitted nuclear-history uncertainty.

The desired output is a complete topology-aware endpoint × environment matrix, not an orientation-only PGLS screen. The six AU-nonrejected local topologies are a starting sensitivity set; raw bootstrap ecology support remains `not_evaluable` where the archived topology bundle does not contain the required trees.

No E1 row becomes a causal result merely by surviving a topology gate.

## E3 — Evolutionary depth stays independent of the current atlas

For discrete authority states:

- orientation: 4–6 minimum changes;
- phyllary posture: 3;
- stickiness: 5.

These histories are estimated without using current environmental associations to choose where a transition should occur.

For continuous image traits, historical analysis remains limited by taxon/morph matching and should not manufacture discrete transitions from weak species-tip means.

## E4 — Historical environment is event-specific, not Azami-predicted by default

Where a dated event and commensurate palaeoenvironmental variable exist, test environmental state, direction, variability and extremes against matched same-duration windows.

The current orientation chronology × palaeolocation envelope remains valid evidence, but its interpretation changes slightly:

- it is one event-specific historical environment analysis within a broader exploratory Chapter 2;
- it is not the central confirmation test of an Azami BIO12 hypothesis;
- the unresolved result remains informative.

Likewise, current RSDS has no directly commensurate PALEO-PGEM historical surface, so colour–radiation history remains unresolved unless a separate defensible historical radiation model is introduced.

## E5 — Azami comparison comes last

After E1–E4 are frozen, compare EAzami with Azami using a separate cross-chapter matrix.

Each homologous relationship should be classified as one of:

1. **concordant** — same trait/environment domain and compatible direction;
2. **discordant** — homologous relationship but opposite/absent direction at the focal scale;
3. **EAzami-only** — Chapter 2 exploratory candidate without an Azami counterpart/support;
4. **Azami-only** — Chapter 1 candidate not recovered in the focal EAzami atlas;
5. **not comparable** — trait ontology, environmental variable or scale is not commensurate.

Azami concordance may strengthen recurrence across scales, but it cannot retroactively determine which EAzami rows are analysed or reported.

## What changes in the manuscript

The paper should no longer open its ecological Results with orientation × hydric and colour × radiation as if those were the predeclared Chapter 2 targets.

A better paper-level sequence is:

### Result 1 — EAzami current environmental atlas

Show the full exploratory trait × environment field, including unsupported and new EAzami-only directions.

### Result 2 — Unequal evolutionary depth

Orientation/phyllary/stickiness repeated histories and shared-localization boundary.

### Result 3 — Trait-specific current ecology after phylogenetic/topology control

Promote only rows justified by the EAzami atlas and its own uncertainty gates.

### Result 4 — Historical event trajectories

Orientation and any additional dated-event/environment combinations that become evaluable.

### Result 5 — Azami–EAzami cross-chapter synthesis

Ask whether global current breadth and focal evolutionary/history depth point to the same environmental domains, different domains, or different directions.

### Result 6 — Whole-capitulum model and public-data ceiling

Retain modular / partial coordinated remodelling only after the complete EAzami field is visible.

## Revised central conclusion target

The target is no longer:

> Azami identified precipitation and radiation candidates, and EAzami tested whether those candidates explain repeated evolution.

It becomes:

> **EAzami independently maps environmental correspondence across capitulum modules, reconstructs how those modules were repeatedly assembled through evolutionary history, and then asks when the resulting focal-radiation patterns agree with or depart from the global spatial patterns in Azami.**

The scientifically interesting outcomes include both agreement and disagreement. A Chapter 2-specific environmental relationship is not a failure to replicate Azami; an Azami relationship absent in EAzami is not evidence that the global result is wrong. They describe different spatial, phylogenetic and historical scales.
