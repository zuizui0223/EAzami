## Naming cleanup

The repository already uses hypothesis labels for substantive biological questions. Those labels are retained:

- **H1** — repeated orientation transitions track the fixed present-niche regime `BIO15 up + BIO1 down`;
- **H2** — the same regime is tracked in the reverse direction for D->U under the declared present-niche estimator;
- **H4** — the present regime does **not** persist across the historical uncertainty envelope of the only calendarized U->D origin event.

The later geography/internal-edge analyses are therefore treated as **robustness tests R1-R4**, not new numbered biological hypotheses. Existing result filenames are retained unchanged for provenance.

## Primary biological spine

repeated mosaic capitulum assembly

## Claim A — mosaic assembly remains the headline

Orientation, phyllary posture and involucre stickiness all require repeated minimum changes within the young radiation, show unequal central relative-depth profiles, and do not repeatedly localize transitions to the same branches. The strongest paired ordering remains:

- phyllary deeper-permissive than stickiness: **1000/1000** topologies;
- phyllary deeper-permissive than orientation: **993/1000**;
- orientation deeper-permissive than stickiness: **905/1000**;
- complete `phyllary < orientation < stickiness`: **898/1000**.

Coverage matching retains the central phyllary-deeper ordering but not strict separation of deep tails.

## Claim B — H1: U->D tracks a fixed composite present-niche regime

The fixed vector is:

```text
BIO15 up + BIO1 down
```

No new climate axis was added after the hypothesis was frozen.

Primary exact results:

- n>=5, 12 taxa: **16/792 = 2.02%**;
- n>=3 sensitivity, 13 taxa: **19/1716 = 1.11%**;
- strict n>=10, 9 taxa: **4/126 = 3.17%**.

Strict single-axis ranks are weaker than the composite:

- BIO15 alone: **7/126 = 5.56%**;
- lower BIO1 alone: **8/126 = 6.35%**;
- composite: **4/126 = 3.17%**.

Interpretation: the supported object is a **composite present-niche regime**, not a precipitation-seasonality-only or temperature-only causal effect.

## Claim C — H2: bidirectional present-niche tracking

Under the same strict 9-taxon panel:

- forward U->D alignment median: **0.320891**;
- reverse D->U alignment median: **0.339529**;
- both directions positive on **6/6** accepted topologies;
- exact bidirectional-floor rank: **3/126 = 2.38%**.

The direction survives deletion of any one strict-panel taxon; exact extremeness is deletion-sensitive. This is bidirectional **present-niche tracking**, not demonstrated genetic reversibility, adaptation, or selection.

## Claim D — H1 robustness R1-R4

### R1 — region and single-taxon dependence

- strict n>=10 passes: **4/126 = 3.17%**;
- Japan-only n>=5 remains directionally positive but is not exceptional: **10/56 = 17.86%**;
- deleting each strict-panel taxon once leaves the U->D composite positive on all six topologies in **9/9** deletion panels;
- the U->D exact <=0.05 criterion survives only **2/9** deletions, so exact extremeness is multi-taxon/deletion-sensitive.

Thus the direction is not generated by one taxon, but the pattern is not a universal Japan-only rule.

### R2 — simple linear geography residualization

After residualizing taxon-centroid BIO15 and BIO1 against `1 + latitude + longitude`:

- strict n>=10 composite remains positive on **6/6** topologies;
- exact rank **5/126 = 3.97%**;
- n>=5 sensitivity **41/792 = 5.18%**, borderline and secondary.

The strict result is not removed by the simplest linear north-south/east-west gradient. This does not remove all spatial or regional confounding.

### R3 — internal-edge-only scoring

With CTMC and Brownian reconstruction on the full pruned tree but terminal child edges excluded from the scored statistic:

- strict n>=10: **3/126 = 2.38%**;
- n>=5 sensitivity: **29/792 = 3.66%**;
- composite positive on **6/6** topologies in both panels.

The signal is therefore not confined to terminal-tip contribution under the declared reconstruction.

### R4 — geography residualization and internal-edge exclusion together

Applying R2 and R3 simultaneously:

- strict n>=10: **3/126 = 2.38%**;
- n>=5 sensitivity: **29/792 = 3.66%**;
- composite positive on **6/6** topologies.

This is the final declared coarse public-data stress test. No further correlated climate predictors or post-result robustness variants should be added to strengthen H1.

## Claim E — H4: present/origin regime persistence is not supported

For the only calendarized core-*Nipponocirsium* U->D event, the current-regime historical sign criterion is:

```text
BIO15 delta > 0
AND
BIO1 delta < 0
```

Across 94 admissible chronology pairs x four palaeolocation scenarios:

- overall match: **99/376 = 26.3%**;
- Taiwan: **20/94 = 21.3%**;
- Ryukyu corridor: **9/94 = 9.6%**;
- southern Japan: **41/94 = 43.6%**;
- East-Asia core corridor: **29/94 = 30.9%**;
- only **6/94** chronologies match in all four regions.

At the central 0.79->0.74 Ma chronology, BIO1 decreases in all four regions but BIO15 also decreases in all four, opposite the present U->D BIO15 direction. The present regime therefore should not be projected backward as the origin trigger.

## Current positive conclusion

> **A multidimensional capitulum was repeatedly and mosaically assembled at unequal evolutionary depths. Orientation additionally shows a distributed East-Asian present transition-niche regime: U<->D transition probabilities track opposite sides of a fixed BIO15/BIO1 niche vector, the U->D direction survives strict coverage, deletion of any one taxon, simple linear geography removal, internal-edge-only scoring and their combined stress, but the same regime is not supported across the historical uncertainty envelope of the only calendarized U->D origin event.**

## What this does and does not establish

Supported:

- repeated mosaic assembly;
- unequal central evolutionary depth;
- scale-partitioned present orientation ecology;
- transition-level present-niche concordance for orientation;
- robustness to the declared R1-R4 falsifications;
- present/origin regime decoupling for the one calendarized event.

Not established:

- BIO15 or BIO1 as a single causal driver;
- historical climate exposure on reconstructed internal branches;
- selection, adaptation, convergence, transition-time fitness;
- rain/wetting, UV, temperature or pollinator mediation in East-Asian *Cirsium*;
- universal Japan-only transition-regime tracking.

## Stop rule

The coarse public-data climate programme is closed after R4. Further causal progress requires **new focal mediator/fitness measurements or event-linked historical data**, not additional correlated BIOCLIM screening.
