# Orientation transition-regime H3 — internal-edge falsification v1

## Question

Is the supported fixed `BIO15 up + BIO1 down` U→D transition-regime correspondence more than a terminal-tip state contrast?

## Fixed test

The symmetric CTMC is fit on the complete panel tree and Brownian environmental reconstruction uses all admitted tip centroids. Only the scoring step changes: any edge whose child is a terminal tip contributes zero. The composite statistic is therefore calculated from **internal edges only**.

Primary panel: strict n>=10 East-Asian panel, 9 taxa, 5 U / 4 D, all 126 count-preserving maps.

Sensitivity: n>=5 East-Asian panel, 12 taxa, 7 U / 5 D, all 792 maps.

## Decision

H3 is supported only if the strict-panel internal-edge composite is positive on all six accepted topologies and its exact one-sided state-map fraction is <=0.05.

## Boundary

Internal environmental values remain Brownian reconstructions from present-day niche centroids. A positive result shows that the branchwise signal is not confined to terminal edges; it does not establish historical environmental exposure, transition age, selection, adaptation, convergence, or mechanism.
