# Chapter 2 repository-wide evidence audit v1

Status date: 2026-08-27

Purpose: identify every EAzami result that can strengthen the Chapter 2 JEB paper without importing a different chapter's estimand or exceeding a frozen claim boundary.

## Main-text evidence

1. **Scale-dependent present-day integration.** The frozen Azami handoff gives a within-taxon registered-module contrast of 0.164502 (95% interval 0.130693–0.179475) and an among-taxon contrast of 0.088475 (0.024942–0.126171) at the >=5 threshold. The >=2 sensitivity preserves the ordering. This defines present-day integration; it is not a historical or genetic module claim.
2. **A strongly asymmetric radiation as context.** Thirty-six of 38 sampled Japanese concepts belong to the dominant radiation under the accepted synthesis. The 36:1:1 occupancy is descriptive, not a diversification-rate estimate.
3. **Repeated discrete-state lower bounds.** Orientation requires 4–6 changes, phyllary posture exactly 3 and stickiness exactly 5 across the frozen topology ensemble.
4. **Recurrence is not transition localization.** Orientation has no individually forced ML edge; the JPN_36 phyllary terminal change is forced in 0.754 of UFBoot trees; stickiness placements remain partial. Stable counts can coexist with uncertain branch identity.
5. **No excess continuous shared branch localization.** The observed ML mean pairwise branch-change rho of 0.408006 has reconstruction-aware P=0.3504. Common tree/reconstruction geometry is sufficient for the apparent broad coupling.
6. **No stable historical module.** The within-minus-between continuous module contrast is unsupported, and discrete pairwise overlap changes sign or includes zero across topology-only sensitivities.

## Background or Discussion evidence

- The authority-backed dominant-radiation sample contains all four observed orientation × stickiness combinations. A colonization history does not map to one capitulum state combination.
- Species-tip compression can erase recent transitions: in the only morph-linked testable colour system, the minimum count changes from 1 to 2 when white and coloured population samples are retained. This is a single-system information-resolution result, not a transition-rate estimate.
- JPN_20 is non-monophyletic in the ML tree and 0/1000 UFBoot trees. It is pruned rather than forcibly collapsed, illustrating the taxon-concept admission problem.
- Broad current climate distance and sparse ploidy records do not provide simple deterministic explanations of capitulum disparity. These are coverage-limited negative constraints, not tests of adaptation.

## Supplementary evidence

- The high-depth global lightness direction does not replicate in the source-balanced Japan7 panel. This is an evidence-provenance contrast, not a rescued colour-history result.
- The failed absolute-time calibration explains why the paper uses substitutions/site, relative branch structure and minimum changes rather than ages or rates per million years.
- Candidate continuous involucre/armature endpoints remain coverage-audit-only because only two concepts reach the >=2 threshold.
- Complete transition-count, root-state and topology distributions should be reported even when the main text uses bounded summaries.

## Excluded from the Chapter 2 estimand

- Functional and fitness meta-analyses, including reproductive herbivory, pollinator/antagonist mosaics and field protocols, belong to the function/fitness chapter.
- Existing v3/v4 covariance simulations model the present phenotype field and belong to Chapter 1 Supplement. They are not evolutionary-history simulations.
- Population genomic origin, plastid, cytotype and causal field experiments are future ancestry/function gates.
- Adaptive radiation, convergence, independent origin, shared developmental mechanism and absolute evolutionary rate remain prohibited conclusions.

## Fail-closed data issues

- JPN_29 cannot enter a clean primary trait-to-tip analysis until specimen identity is resolved. The original eight-concept reconstruction result remains frozen; a single JPN_29-excluded rerun is labelled post-outcome provenance sensitivity and cannot rescue the failed headline.
- Global species-level photograph summaries must be described as species proxies, not phenotypes observed on the sequenced Japanese individuals.
- Non-detection of phylogenetic structure must not be translated into zero signal, proven lability or evolutionary independence.

## Recommended JEB spine

> Present-day capitulum integration is detectable but scale dependent. Several discrete states recur, yet recurrence counts, exact transition placement and shared branch localization are distinct. Once common reconstruction geometry and topology uncertainty are propagated, the data do not support one persistent historical capitulum module.
