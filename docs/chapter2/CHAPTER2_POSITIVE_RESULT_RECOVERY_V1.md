# Chapter 2 positive-result recovery v1

Status date: 2026-09-03

Machine-readable ledger: `data/evidence/chapter2_positive_result_recovery_v1.csv`

## Decision

The repository-wide audit does **not** support a manuscript whose emotional or logical center is `no historical trigger found`. The stronger evidence hierarchy is:

`dominant-radiation configuration diversity -> repeated component changes -> unequal relative evolutionary depth -> incomplete/split ecological correspondence -> historical-cause identifiability ceiling`.

The negative trigger result remains important, but it is the **last discrimination step**, not the first biological result.

## 1. Main-paper positive core

The main manuscript should retain only the positive results that directly answer the Chapter 2 historical-assembly question.

### A. Diversity is concentrated within one young radiation

- 36/38 sampled Japanese paper concepts occur in the dominant radiation.
- The dominant radiation contains multiple authority-backed capitulum configurations rather than one syndrome.
- Present public-image and environmental screens show large disparity within that radiation, so separate colonization history is not a monotonic proxy for phenotype or broad current environment.

Use the 36/38 and configuration result as main context. Keep the nine-taxon continuous disparity values concise or in Supporting Information because they are based on a smaller public-image subset.

### B. Three component traits each changed repeatedly

- orientation: ML minimum 6; UFBoot 4-6;
- phyllary posture: exactly 3;
- stickiness: exactly 5.

These are minimum-change lower bounds, not independent origins.

### C. Their historical depth geometry differs

Frozen median UFBoot envelopes:

- orientation: 0.795-0.994;
- phyllary: 0.695-1.000;
- stickiness: 0.937-0.954.

Stickiness is shallow and tightly bounded; phyllary admits substantially deeper histories; orientation is intermediate/mixed. PR #160 now quantifies this ordering directly on the same 1,000 bootstrap topologies so that the claim no longer relies only on marginal medians.

### D. The capitulum does not repeatedly change as one synchronized syndrome

Zero of three discrete trait pairs passes the robust shared-transition-localization rule. This supports a **mosaic historical assembly** interpretation while stopping short of genetic/developmental modularity.

## 2. Ecological support to restore selectively

The old V5 ecology should not retake control of the paper, but it should not be erased either.

### Orientation is ecologically organized, but not on one biological scale

The frozen East-Asian state contrast has stable BIO15-positive and BIO1-negative directions. PR #159 adds a cleaner cross-scale result using the exact Azami within/among artifact:

- BIO12: among-taxon supported, within-taxon unsupported;
- BIO1: within-taxon supported, among-taxon unsupported;
- BIO15: stable East-Asian state contrast without a matching positive within-taxon association.

If #159 validates, this belongs in the main Discussion and possibly one compact Results paragraph because it explains why `current ecological correspondence` cannot simply be projected backward as one repeated historical cause.

### White-coloured sister systems

Two dated sister systems independently show lower chroma and higher lightness in white lineages and repeat three coarse geometry directions. Keep this in Supporting Information or a compact Discussion bridge. It supports partial remodelling but is not required for the three-trait historical core.

## 3. Mechanistic positives that should not overload this paper

The following are real positive repository results but are better treated as mechanism priors or companion analyses:

- public *C. nipponicum* genome: candidate homologs recovered for 11/11 reviewed flavonoid query anchors;
- public *C. takaoense* RNA-seq: DFR- and ANS-homologous reads recovered;
- direct Cirsium reproductive-herbivory meta: reduced-herbivory/ambient seed-output RR=2.674, 95% CI 2.388-2.993;
- pollinator dependence high in 5/6 Cirsium studies and multi-agent experiments support a selection mosaic rather than one universal agent;
- external orientation mechanism reduction identifies time-window pollination + abiotic protection as the only 5/5 core-matching family.

These results establish that plausible functional/biotic mechanisms exist in the real world and are empirically testable. They do **not** establish the mechanism that generated the focal historical transitions. They should therefore remain Supporting/companion/Chapter-3 priors rather than become Chapter-2 causal claims.

## 4. Simulation positives are auxiliary

The held-out support geometry is not reproduced by the simple frozen null (0/64), and an among-taxon structural addition improves fit in 22/24 diagnostic replicates. Draft PR #122 further reports successful v4.1 scale-decoupling families.

These are useful for the dissertation-wide generative model, but the Chapter 2 empirical paper does not need them for its historical-assembly claim. Keep them auxiliary unless an independent held-out validation converts the v4.1 result into a fully frozen result.

## 5. Historical environment remains the terminal discrimination layer

The central 0.79-0.74 Ma orientation chronology does contain a coherent descriptive climate trajectory, and PR #158 quantifies relative regional ordering inside the uncertainty envelope. These tendencies are worth reporting as tendencies.

However:

- no signed BIO1/BIO4/BIO12/BIO15 direction survives the full chronology x palaeolocation envelope;
- no level/change/variability metric survives the full gate;
- no orientation global sea-level metric survives;
- lineage-level climate diagnostic: 0/324 robust event classes;
- multi-lineage sea-level diagnostic: 0/21 robust event-metric classes.

Therefore the paper ends with a genuine information asymmetry:

> We can reconstruct **what differentiated and roughly how deep in the radiation those changes can lie** much more strongly than we can reconstruct **one repeated historical cause**.

## 6. V7 manuscript order

### Result 1 — diversity to be explained

Dominant-radiation context + multiple capitulum configurations. Continuous public-image/environment disparity stays concise.

### Result 2 — repeated historical assembly

Minimum-change counts for orientation, phyllary and stickiness.

### Result 3 — temporal stratification / mosaic assembly

Relative-depth envelopes + paired-topology depth-ordering robustness from PR #160 + 0/3 shared-localization result.

### Result 4 — ecological reach is scale-dependent

If #159 validates, insert the cross-scale orientation environment result here. Otherwise retain the frozen East-Asian state contrast only as supporting context.

### Result 5 — calendar-time and historical-cause boundary

One bounded orientation event, central climate tendency, uncertainty-envelope failure, broader 0/324 climate and 0/21 sea-level results.

## 7. Preferred title direction

Primary candidate:

> **Repeated mosaic assembly at unequal evolutionary depths in a young thistle radiation**

Alternative emphasizing evidence hierarchy:

> **Repeated capitulum differentiation is more identifiable than its historical causes in a young thistle radiation**

Do not lead the title with `without a recurring trigger`; that makes the strongest positive history look like setup for a null result.

## 8. Conclusion to preserve

The paper should end on this logic:

> A young Japanese *Cirsium* radiation contains multiple capitulum configurations assembled through repeated changes in orientation, phyllary posture and involucre stickiness. These traits differ in the depth and localization of their minimum histories and do not repeatedly share one synchronized transition pattern. Present ecological correspondence is detectable but scale-dependent, whereas calendar-time linkage and historical exposure are much less identifiable. The result is therefore not an absence of ecological structure, but a separation between a well-resolved history of phenotypic assembly and a less-resolved history of cause.

## 9. Hard boundaries

Do not promote:

- minimum changes -> independent origins/convergence;
- relative lineage depth -> calendar age;
- present environment -> historical selection;
- candidate pathway homologs -> pigment mechanism;
- literature fitness effects -> focal-trait adaptation;
- global sea level -> local land bridges;
- unresolved historical trigger -> environmental irrelevance.
