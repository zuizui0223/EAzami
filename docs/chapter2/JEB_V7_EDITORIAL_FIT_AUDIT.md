# JEB V7 editorial-fit audit

Status date: 2026-09-03  
Journal: *Journal of Evolutionary Biology* (JEB)  
Official scope/guidelines checked: https://academic.oup.com/jeb/pages/author-guidelines

## Bottom line

**JEB remains a defensible primary target if V7 is submitted as an evolutionary-assembly paper, not as a palaeoclimate null-result paper.**

The journal explicitly prioritizes robust studies that advance understanding of evolutionary process and provide insights that can generalize beyond one taxon. It accepts robust negative results, but V7 should not rely on the negative historical-trigger result for novelty.

The strongest general evolutionary contribution is:

> A multidimensional phenotype can undergo repeated change within a young radiation while its components occupy different evolutionary depths, fail to share one synchronized transition history and retain present ecological correspondence at different biological scales.

The historical-trigger ceiling then demonstrates the different identifiability requirements of `assembly` versus `cause`.

## What JEB can reasonably see as new

### 1. Positive historical architecture of a complex phenotype

The paper does not treat the capitulum as one syndrome. Three authority-defined components are reconstructed separately through the same young radiation:

- orientation: ML 6, UFBoot 4–6;
- phyllary posture: exactly 3;
- stickiness: exactly 5.

Their depth envelopes differ sharply and 0/3 trait pairs pass robust shared-transition localization.

**Generalizable insight:** phenotypic integration in an organism does not require a single synchronized macroevolutionary assembly history.

### 2. Evolutionary depth adds information beyond transition count

The useful result is not `same lability, different depth`; equal lability is not identified.

The defensible contribution is:

> Topological depth geometry contains information not reducible to the raw minimum-change count or a simple coverage-adjusted burden.

Orientation and phyllary have the same simple ML steps/resolved-concept ratio (0.300) yet different depth profiles, while stickiness is more step-dense and substantially shallower.

### 3. Present ecological organization is scale-partitioned

For orientation:

- BIO12 is among-taxon only;
- BIO1 is within-taxon only in Azami and differs in direction from the East-Asian state contrast;
- BIO15 has a stable East-Asian state contrast without a matching positive within-taxon response.

**Generalizable insight:** an ecological association at one biological level should not automatically be treated as a lineage-level evolutionary response coefficient.

### 4. Assembly and historical cause have different identifiability ceilings

Repeated component history and relative depth are recoverable across phylogenetic uncertainty, whereas only one current capitulum event reaches the full calendar-time/palaeolocation/environment chain.

The 0/324 climate and 0/21 sea-level outcomes are valuable because they close a predeclared coarse-trigger family after uncertainty propagation; they are not the primary biological discovery.

## Main editorial risks

### Risk A — `This is mostly public-data synthesis/reanalysis`

Expected concern: the study does not add a new field experiment or new sequencing campaign.

**Defence:**

- the independent Japan38 compatibility reconstruction and topology-ensemble propagation create a new historical test, rather than merely collating published values;
- the study joins non-exchangeable evidence layers under explicit claim boundaries;
- the main novelty is an evolutionary inference about multidimensional assembly, not a database description.

**Manuscript requirement:** Results/Fig. 2 must carry the paper. Long provenance detail belongs in SI.

### Risk B — `Relative lineage depth is not time`

Expected concern: the title says evolutionary depths while the scaffold is undated.

**Defence:**

- the metric is explicitly topology-only descendant-lineage depth;
- terminal = 1 and lower values mean broader descendant lineages;
- no Ma or rate interpretation is made;
- the separate calendar-time section demonstrates why topology-only depth and absolute time are deliberately not conflated.

**Manuscript requirement:** Every axis/caption must say `topology only, not time`.

### Risk C — `Three traits are too heterogeneous for cross-trait comparison`

Expected concern: orientation/stickiness are binary whereas phyllary is four-state, with different coverage.

**Defence:**

- V7 no longer claims equal lability;
- minimum-step burden and evolutionary rate are not compared as one common parameter;
- the primary estimand is each trait's exact depth envelope among its own globally minimum histories and topology-wise ordering sensitivity.

**Manuscript requirement:** retain the turnover-burden caveat near the first depth result, not only in Limitations.

### Risk D — `0/3 localization is not evidence of modularity`

Expected concern: absence of synchronized transitions could be over-read as genetic/developmental modules.

**Defence:**

- the manuscript explicitly uses `mosaic historical assembly`, not genetic/developmental modularity;
- introgression, ancestral polymorphism, correlated selection and shared development remain compatible with the result.

### Risk E — `Current ecology and historical ecology are mixed`

Expected concern: BIO12/BIO15/BIO1 present associations may be used to imply historical selection.

**Defence:**

- Fig. 3 labels within, among and East-Asian state estimands separately;
- no pooling occurs;
- historical climate is tested only after the separate chronology/palaeolocation gate.

### Risk F — `The historical-trigger section is a large multiple-testing exercise`

Expected concern: 17 BIOCLIM × multiple contexts could look like fishing.

**Defence:**

- it is a terminal robustness/closure analysis with frozen gates and matched backgrounds;
- 0/324 is not used to claim environmental irrelevance;
- V7 explicitly stops further broad-proxy expansion (PR #157 closed before outcome inspection).

**Manuscript requirement:** keep Fig. 5 compact; move full atlas detail to SI.

### Risk G — `Mechanisms are not measured in the focal history`

Expected concern: pollinators/antagonists/abiotic protection are discussed without ancestry-matched experiments.

**Defence:**

- mechanism evidence is routed as `MECHANISTIC_PRIOR` / companion evidence;
- the paper does not call the focal transitions adaptive;
- Chapter 3 / future work is clearly identified as the place for trait -> function -> fitness experiments.

## What should be in the cover letter

1. The paper's first sentence should state the **general problem of multidimensional phenotypic assembly**, not the absence of a climate trigger.
2. State that the same topology ensemble is used to propagate uncertainty across three trait histories.
3. Highlight the scale-partitioned ecology result as an independent reason not to collapse current association into historical cause.
4. Mention the historical-trigger analyses only as a stringent final discrimination layer.
5. Explicitly say that no adaptation or independent-convergence claim is made.
6. Disclose generative-AI assistance as required by current JEB guidelines.

## Suggested one-paragraph editorial pitch

> Complex structures are often analysed as integrated syndromes, yet present integration need not imply a synchronized evolutionary history. Using a young *Cirsium* radiation, we reconstruct three capitulum components across the same phylogenetic uncertainty ensemble and show repeated change at unequal topology-defined evolutionary depths with no robust shared transition-localization pattern. Present orientation–environment correspondence is additionally partitioned across within- and among-taxon scales. By separately propagating calendar-time, palaeolocation and palaeoenvironmental uncertainty, we show that this positive history of phenotypic assembly is much more identifiable than one recurring historical cause. The study therefore provides an empirical framework for separating the assembly of multidimensional phenotypes from retrospective causal attribution in rapid radiations.

## Recommendation

Keep JEB as the primary target through V7 freeze.

Reconsider the target only if one of the following happens:

- #160 fails to reproduce the depth ordering and the unequal-depth headline becomes substantially weaker;
- manuscript word/figure compression forces the assembly result to become secondary to data-provenance detail;
- the final figures cannot communicate a general evolutionary result beyond *Cirsium* specialists.

If #160 validates, the current title and five-figure architecture are well aligned with the journal's evolutionary-biology scope.
