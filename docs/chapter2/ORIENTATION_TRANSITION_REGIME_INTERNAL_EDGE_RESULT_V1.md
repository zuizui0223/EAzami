## Interpretation

The supported East-Asian transition-regime concordance is **not merely a terminal state–environment contrast** under the declared reconstruction. It remains visible in the inferred internal branch structure.

This is stronger than comparing extant U and D taxon means, but it is still not direct historical environmental evidence. Internal environmental values are Brownian reconstructions from present-day niche centroids, and the CTMC gives transition probabilities rather than observed ancestral events.

Accordingly, this result supports **internal-branch phylogenetic concordance** with the fixed composite regime; it does not establish transition-time climate, causation, selection, adaptation, convergence, or mechanism.
