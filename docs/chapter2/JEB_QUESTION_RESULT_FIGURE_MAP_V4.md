## Display gates

- Generate numeric panels only from declared frozen artifacts.
- Show exact depth envelopes; do not average or weight equally parsimonious histories.
- Do not label relative lineage-depth as event age, recent, ancient or rate.
- Do not label minimum changes as independent origins, convergence or adaptation.
- Do not rewrite `not_evaluable` as zero effect or no relationship.
- Do not call the orientation climate pattern adaptation or historical event matching.
