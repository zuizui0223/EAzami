## Central result

> **A complex reproductive phenotype is repeatedly reassembled rather than preserving one synchronized historical organization, and its ecological correspondence is not invariant across biological scale or phenotype representation.**

### Historical axis

- orientation: repeated, 4–6 minimum changes;
- phyllary posture: repeated, exactly 3;
- stickiness: repeated, exactly 5;
- paired depth ordering strongly favors phyllary deeper, stickiness shallower;
- 0/3 trait pairs show robust repeated shared transition localization.

### Ecological axis

Common9 does not recover one shared abiotic syndrome.

Orientation shows different environmental structure at different levels:

- static East-Asian D/U classes: weak;
- EAzami reconstructed transitions: BIO15 up + BIO1 down;
- Azami among taxa: BIO12 association;
- Azami within taxa: supported BIO1 association in the opposite direction from EAzami transition BIO1.

Therefore:

`orientation_environment_correspondence_is_scale_and_representation_dependent`
