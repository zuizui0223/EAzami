# Chapter 2 trait-cause falsification v1

Status date: 2026-09-05

This result does **not** search for additional predictors. It freezes a small competing-model set for phyllary posture and stickiness and asks which models are contradicted, weakened, compatible, or not evaluable under existing Chapter 2 evidence.

## Phyllary posture

Current history: exactly 3 minimum changes; JPN36 (*Cirsium sieboldii*) is appressed and is forced on its terminal edge on the ML reconstruction, with UFBoot forced fraction 0.728.

Three fixed models were compared.

1. **Pollinator-access primary.** Weakened. In the direct *Pedicularis rex* protective-bract manipulation, legitimate pollinator visitation did not respond to treatment (beta=0.012, p=0.958). This is a nonhomologous analog and therefore does not directly test *Cirsium* phyllary geometry.
2. **Reproductive-enemy protection.** Current leading external mechanism prior. In the same manipulation, intact protective bracts were associated with lower seed predation (published-model beta=-0.072, p<0.0001), higher final seed set (beta=+0.025, p<0.0001), and no pollinator-visitation effect.
3. **Broad abiotic/geographic sorting.** Not supported in the current public panel. The broader state-diverse occurrence panel contains only four taxa (3 ascending, 1 appressed) and gives spatial-segregation p=0.4998.

**Current reading:** reproductive-enemy protection outranks pollinator-access benefit and simple broad geographic sorting as the specific mechanism prior, but focal *Cirsium* causation remains untested. Direct wetting protection also remains not evaluable.

**Next falsifier:** reversible damage-free JPN36 appressed-phyllary access/protection manipulation versus sham, jointly measuring enemy attack, legitimate pollinator access/effective transfer, wetting and mature viable seed.

## Stickiness

Current history: exactly 5 minimum changes and a shallow median relative-depth envelope 0.937–0.954. JPN06 is nonsticky/nearly nonsticky and is forced on its terminal edge in 0.995 of current UFBoot trees. Sticky JPN15 and nonsticky JPN06 are a canonical 100/100 ML sister contrast.

Three fixed models were compared.

1. **Universal antagonist defence.** Contradicted as a universal rule. Direct neutralization of sticky inflorescence exudate in *Cirsium discolor* did not increase seed predators and did not reduce successful seed production.
2. **Universal trait cost.** Contradicted as a universal rule. In *Bejaria resinosa*, intact sticky flowers had lower florivory (risk difference -0.21) and higher fruit set (derived RR=1.48148) than washed flowers.
3. **Enemy-dependent defence–cost balance.** Compatible and the only frozen model not contradicted. Existing functional evidence contains all three expected contexts: benefit in *Bejaria*, direct null in *Cirsium discolor*, and cost in *Datura wrightii* under herbivore exclusion (first-year seed production about 53% lower; finite rate of increase 13% lower in sticky plants).

The balanced broader spatial panel (6 sticky, 6 nonsticky taxa) does not show simple broad spatial segregation (p=0.1306), which is compatible with a local interaction mosaic but does not test antagonist communities.

**Current reading:** the best remaining causal domain is local antagonist pressure balanced against the cost of producing/expressing stickiness, not universal defence and not universal cost.

**Next falsifier:** within-JPN15 neutralization of natural stickiness versus sham, measuring enemy access/damage, pollinator effective transfer, treatment side-effects and mature viable seed. JPN06 adhesive restoration remains a secondary sufficiency test only after material-equivalence validation.

## Chapter 2 consequence

The trait-specific causal map can now be stated more concretely:

- **orientation:** composite hydric/thermal present-niche regime; direct historical cause still unresolved;
- **phyllary posture:** reproductive-enemy protection is the leading mechanism prior, while pollinator-access and broad spatial models are weakened/not supported;
- **stickiness:** a local enemy-pressure × trait-cost selection mosaic is the only frozen model not contradicted by the current benefit/null/cost evidence pattern.

These are model-elimination results and mechanism priors. They do not establish adaptation or focal East-Asian *Cirsium* causal effects without direct manipulation and reproductive-fitness endpoints.
