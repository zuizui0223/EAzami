## Figure 4 — Present ecological tracking does not identify the origin regime

**Question:** Does the present orientation regime persist at the only bounded historical U->D origin event?

Panels:

### 4A. Current regime hypothesis
Simple vector diagram: U->D corresponds to `BIO15 up + BIO1 down`; D->U corresponds to the reverse.

### 4B. Historical gate
Core-*Nipponocirsium* event; central 0.79–0.74 Ma; 94 chronology pairs x 4 regions = 376 scenarios.

### 4C. H4 match fractions
- Taiwan 21.3%;
- Ryukyu 9.6%;
- southern Japan 43.6%;
- East-Asia core 30.9%;
- overall 99/376 = 26.3%;
- frozen support line at 75%.

### 4D. Central chronology mismatch
BIO1 decreases in all four regions but BIO15 also decreases in all four, so 0/4 central regional scenarios match the current U->D sign regime.

### 4E. Small boundary inset
- only one capitulum event reaches calendar + palaeolocation + environment;
- broader climate atlas 0/324;
- sea-level diagnostic 0/21.

**Claim:** present orientation–niche tracking is not a valid proxy for the origin environment of the bounded transition; current ecological organization and origin-time historical environment are decoupled under current evidence.
