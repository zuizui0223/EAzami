# Chapter 2 to Chapter 3 RAD-seq bridge v1

## Decision

Chapter 2 is a complete public-evidence paper. Chapter 3 is the own-data test of the histories that Chapter 2 cannot localize uniquely.

```text
published Comp1061 ensemble + authority states
                  |
                  v
  Chapter 2 recurrence and placement uncertainty
                  |
          inverse sample selection
                  |
                  v
Japan-wide own RAD-seq sensitivity + same-individual traits
                  |
                  v
  retained, revised or falsified Chapter 2 histories
```

The arrow does not run backwards. Chapter 3 is not required for Chapter 2 validity, and a Chapter 3 topology cannot retroactively establish adaptation.

The exact fields, evidence paths and claim boundaries are frozen in `data/evidence/chapter2_to_chapter3_sampling_priorities_v1.csv`.

## RAD-seq product boundary

The user-owned Japan-wide RAD-seq tree is scientifically useful as an own-data sensitivity and population-history product. It is admitted as a cross-species phylogeny only when:

- independently collected replicates agree;
- sufficient homologous loci are shared across the compared concepts;
- restriction-site dropout does not determine major clades;
- cytotypes are not forced through one diploid caller;
- homeolog/paralog collapse is screened;
- population structure, admixture and network alternatives are retained;
- a strict-locus sensitivity does not reverse the focal conclusion.

If these conditions fail, report population ancestry or within-cytotype structure and retain the Comp1061 target-capture scaffold for cross-species history. This failure does not reopen Chapter 2.

## Same-individual package

Every own-data individual needs one immutable identifier linking RAD tissue, voucher, population, deidentified locality, orientation, phyllary posture, stickiness, cytotype status and authorization records. A taxon-level authority state cannot silently replace a missing individual phenotype.

## Rights and conservation

This bridge authorizes no collection. Sampling is blocked unless current occurrence, population census, land access, collection permission, conservation review and taxon/tissue/count-specific conditions are documented. Sensitive coordinates and permit documents remain outside the public repository; only deidentified record IDs enter the analysis ledger.

JPN36 field-manipulation authorization and DNA-collection authorization are separate. The JPN15 and JPN06 conservation signals require current site-specific review and cannot be overridden by scientific priority.

## Claim boundary

Chapter 2 estimates conditional historical recurrence and placement. Chapter 3 estimates own-data topology, ancestry and linked phenotype within its quality gates. Neither a sister contrast nor a revised transition placement establishes mechanism, fitness benefit, convergence or adaptation; those require separately controlled experiments and claim contracts.
