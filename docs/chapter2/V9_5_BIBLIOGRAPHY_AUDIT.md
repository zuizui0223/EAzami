# V9.5 bibliography audit

## Purpose
This file freezes the first-pass bibliography synchronization for the active V9.5 organization-first manuscript. It starts from sources that are already linked to frozen EAzami evidence and then verifies bibliographic metadata externally where needed. It is not yet the final JEB-formatted reference list.

## Core phylogeny / radiation reference

  - manuscript role: Japanese / global *Cirsium* nuclear phylogenomic scaffold, rapid Japanese radiation, gene-tree discordance context.

## Orientation functional evidence

- Niu, Y. & Sun, H. (2013). **A nodding capitulum enhances the reproductive success of Cremanthodium campanulatum (Asteraceae) at high elevations in the Sino-Himalayan Mountains.** *Plant Ecology & Diversity* 6:487–494. DOI: `10.1080/17550874.2012.702793`.
  - manuscript role: closest direct capitulum-angle manipulation; nodding vs experimentally erect heads, water/UV-B pollen-vulnerability measurements, achene outcome, no detected static pollinator orientation preference.

## Cirsium reproductive-herbivory synthesis

- Louda, S.M. & Potvin, M.A. (1995). **Effect of inflorescence-feeding insects on the demography and lifetime fitness of a native plant.** *Ecology* 76:229–245. DOI: `10.2307/1940645`.
  - manuscript role: one independent *Cirsium canescens* data-generation programme in the four-programme synthesis.
  - numerical provenance note: the means/SEs used in the frozen meta-analysis were recovered from Maron et al. (2002) Table 1 rather than asserted as a direct transcription from the 1995 article.

- Maron, J.L., Combs, J.K. & Louda, S.M. (2002). **Convergent demographic effects of insect attack on related thistles in coastal vs. continental dunes.** *Ecology* 83:3382–3392. DOI: `10.1890/0012-9658(2002)083[3382:CDEOIA]2.0.CO;2`.
  - manuscript role: direct *Cirsium occidentale* insect-exclusion seed-output data and secondary numerical source for the older Louda–Potvin means.

- West, N.M. & Louda, S.M. (2018). **Cumulative herbivory outpaces compensation for early floral damage on a monocarpic perennial thistle.** *Oecologia* 186:495–506. DOI: `10.1007/s00442-017-4027-9`.
  - manuscript role: independent *Cirsium canescens* experimental programme.

- Russell, F.L., Taylor, M.R. & Louda, S.M. (2025). **Microsite availability, not floral herbivory, limits recruitment in peripheral native thistle populations.** *Ecosphere* 16:e70310. DOI: `10.1002/ecs2.70310`.
  - manuscript role: independent recent *Cirsium canescens* experimental programme in the frozen synthesis.

## Submission rule
A reference enters the final JEB bibliography only if:
1. it is cited in V9.5 main text or an embedded figure/table legend;
2. its metadata are verified against a primary/publisher/indexed source;
3. its biological role matches the frozen evidence boundary;
4. secondary numerical provenance is stated where applicable.

Do not expand the bibliography simply to make the paper look broader. The purpose is to make the organization-first argument auditable and correctly sourced.
