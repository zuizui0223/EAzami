## What is actually resolved

### 1. Historical organization

Orientation, phyllary posture and stickiness all repeatedly change, occupy unequal evolutionary depths and do not repeatedly localize to the same branches.

This is the main positive Chapter 2 result.

### 2. The three traits do not share one current abiotic template

The frozen common9 screen does not recover one common climatic signature:

- orientation: weak as a static D/U tip-state split;
- stickiness: exploratory wetness/precipitation lead only;
- phyllary: public state replication remains insufficient.

### 3. Orientation ecology is explicitly scale-partitioned

The already-frozen scale audit shows:

- BIO12 annual precipitation: Azami **among-taxon only** (+0.30436; q=0.00640), within-taxon unsupported;
- BIO1 annual mean temperature: Azami **within-taxon only** (+0.01715; q=0.0349), while East-Asian downward-minus-upward is negative;
- BIO15 precipitation seasonality: East-Asian downward-minus-upward is +1.320 to +1.330 SD with strong sign stability, but Azami within-taxon is weakly negative and unsupported after FDR.

Thus the same orientation component does not carry one scale-invariant climate coefficient.

### 4. Transition representation contains information absent from the static comparison

The fixed U->D BIO15-up/BIO1-down transition regime remains exceptional under the frozen finite-map analyses, whereas static common9 orientation separation is weak.

The ecological signal therefore depends not only on taxonomic scale but also on **how evolutionary phenotype is represented**.

## What the held-out programme added

The held-out programme was useful even though it did not produce a confirming coefficient.

### External taxon V1

Strict public occurrence QC left one D and one U lineage. Not evaluable.

### Source-complete V2

A complete source census recovered 46/46 Flora of China taxa and produced a frozen 13-taxon orientation panel (3 D, 10 U). Under the preregistered GBIF QC, only *Cirsium vulgare* survived:

- D = 0 taxa;
- U = 1 taxon.

The external test therefore stopped **before BIO1/BIO15 extraction**.

This is an occurrence-metadata resolution ceiling, not an ecological null.

### Azami as an alternative P3

Azami cannot be relabelled as held-out evidence:

- the relevant climate outcomes were already analyzed;
- its angle is image-vertical referenced, not directly gravity measured;
- its within-taxon BIO1/BIO15 directions do not reproduce the EAzami transition vector.

It remains valuable **existing cross-scale evidence**, not prospective confirmation.

## Why a separate public-data ecology paper is no longer the best split

A new manuscript using the same frozen evidence would substantially duplicate the biological result already carrying Chapter 2:

- historical mosaic assembly;
- common9 trait differences;
- orientation scale partition;
- transition-level ecology;
- causal boundary.

The one feature that would have made a separate paper clearly stronger—independent external confirmation—cannot be supplied by the current public occurrence metadata under the frozen rules.

Splitting now would therefore separate mutually dependent evidence rather than create an independent ecological test.

## Revised Chapter 2 ecological claim

The strongest safe ecological statement is:

> **Ecological correspondence of capitulum orientation is not scale invariant. Static state, within-taxon variation, among-taxon differentiation and reconstructed transitions expose different environmental structure.**

Across the whole capitulum:

> **Repeated historical reassembly is accompanied by trait- and scale-specific ecological correspondence rather than one persistent whole-capitulum syndrome.**

This remains correspondence, not adaptation.

## Stop rule

For the current public-data paper programme:

- no new external-taxon rescue panel;
- no relaxation of the <=10 km occurrence QC;

Future work proceeds only by a genuinely new measurement or causal estimand.
