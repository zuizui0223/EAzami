# V9.5 figure map and JEB submission gate

## Manuscript role
Active manuscript: `MANUSCRIPT_JEB_V9_5_ORGANIZATION_SPINE.md`.

Headline novelty: repeated evolution within one complex reproductive phenotype is **historically stratified**. The manuscript should visually prove three things in order: repeated change, unequal evolutionary depth, and lack of synchronized branch localization. Ecology is a worked extension, not the headline.

## Main figures

### Figure 1 — One young radiation, three repeatedly changing capitulum components
**Purpose:** establish the biological system and show that repeated differentiation occurs inside the same radiation.

**Panel A:** nuclear phylogram / dominant Japanese radiation, with the 38 concepts and unresolved states shown transparently.

**Panel B:** aligned tip-state tracks for orientation, phyllary posture and involucre stickiness. Use identical taxon order and explicit missing-state symbols.

**Panel C:** compact recurrence summary across 1,000 topologies: orientation 4–6 changes, phyllary exactly 3, stickiness exactly 5.

**Claim carried:** repeated component evolution occurs within one young radiation; these are lower bounds on state change, not independent adaptive origins.

### Figure 2 — Repeated histories occupy unequal evolutionary depths
**Purpose:** carry the strongest new result.

**Panel A:** distributions or interval summaries of relative lineage depth for the three traits across the 1,000 topology ensemble.

**Panel B:** paired topology-wise contrasts, reporting the actual ordering counts:
- phyllary deeper-permissive than stickiness: 1000/1000;
- phyllary deeper-permissive than orientation: 993/1000;
- orientation deeper-permissive than stickiness: 905/1000;
- complete central ordering phyllary < orientation < stickiness: 898/1000.

**Panel C:** coverage-matched masking sensitivity, showing that the central phyllary-deeper result survives equalized state coverage while strict deepest tails overlap.

**Claim carried:** recurrence is historically stratified; change counts alone miss the organization of repeated evolution.

### Figure 3 — Components do not repeatedly change on the same branches
**Purpose:** distinguish mosaic reassembly from one synchronized whole-capitulum history.

**Panel A:** branch-wise transition-probability or excess maps for the three traits on one common reference topology.

**Panel B:** 3-pair localization matrix with the branch-length-aware estimate and topology-only sensitivity side by side.

**Panel C:** binary summary showing 0/3 trait pairs pass the robust shared-transition-localization rule.

**Claim carried:** unequal depth is accompanied by lack of repeated branch synchronization; the capitulum is repeatedly reassembled rather than shifted as one historical unit.

### Figure 4 — Ecological correspondence depends on phenotype representation
**Purpose:** provide one ecological worked example without letting climate dominate the paper.

**Panel A:** common9 static state comparison for the three traits, emphasizing that no single shared abiotic niche signature is recovered. Show omnibus finite-map ranks and state/sample counts; flag phyllary as resolution-limited rather than null.

**Panel B:** orientation static state versus transition-level comparison. Contrast the weak common9 static orientation result (2138/6188 = 34.55%) with the transition-level BIO15-up/BIO1-down finite-map ranks (16/792 = 2.02%; 19/1716 = 1.11%; strict 4/126 = 3.17%).

**Panel C:** compact robustness strip for orientation: 9/9 deletion directions retained, geography/internal-edge stresses, and source-partition diagnostic. Do not promote Japan/Taiwan as biological regions; label this as a data-source/geographic confounding diagnostic.

**Claim carried:** ecological information can depend on whether phenotype is represented as extant state or reconstructed evolutionary change.

## Claim discipline
Main-text headline claims should be limited to:
1. repeated change of all three traits within the radiation;
2. robust paired differences in relative evolutionary depth;
3. 0/3 robust shared transition localization;
4. representation-dependent ecology demonstrated most clearly by orientation.

Do not promote as headline claims:
- independent adaptive origins;
- historical climate causation;
- a universal stickiness precipitation law;
- phyllary environmental null effects;
- direct focal `Cirsium` causation for orientation/phyllary/stickiness.
