## Interpretation boundary

This is a deterministic cross-study chronology × paleolocation scenario envelope, not a joint chronology posterior or ancestral-area posterior. It tests whether historical environmental trajectory direction is robust to those public-data uncertainties. It does not establish selection, adaptation, convergence or an exact transition date.
