# EAzami V10 → aza3 causal programme

Status: prospective field-ecology handoff  
Date: 2026-10-04

## Why aza3 exists

EAzami establishes that a young *Cirsium* radiation repeatedly rebuilt the capitulum from orientation, phyllary posture and stickiness with unequal evolutionary histories. It also narrows each component to a different candidate ecological interface.

EAzami does **not** establish that any focal East-Asian state is an adaptation.

aza3 therefore tests the missing causal links:

`trait state → ecological mediator → biotic/abiotic process → reproductive fitness`

All three modules terminate in the same primary fitness currency: mature/filled viable achenes.

## Shared causal architecture

For every module, measure four levels separately:

1. **manipulated trait** — randomized treatment versus sham/control;
2. **mechanistic mediator** — the physical or community process the trait is predicted to change;
3. **interaction/process endpoint** — pollination, antagonist attack or reproductive-tissue damage;
4. **fitness endpoint** — filled/viable achenes, with seed mass as secondary where feasible.

A treatment effect on seeds without the predicted mediator is not counted as confirmation of the proposed mechanism.

## P01 — Orientation × rain/wetting

### EAzami prior

Reconstructed U→D transitions align with BIO15 up + BIO1 down under the frozen transition estimator. External functional evidence makes abiotic reproductive exposure plus timing the leading orientation mechanism. Historical climate does not identify the origin trigger.

### Causal question

> Does downward orientation protect reproductive function specifically during wetting/rain exposure, producing a context-dependent fitness advantage?

### Design

Randomized reversible manipulation within a naturally downward focal *Cirsium* population:

- natural/downward control;
- experimentally reoriented upward;
- sham-handled downward.

If feasible, use paired heads within plant only when treatment spillover and resource coupling are negligible; otherwise one experimental head per plant with blocked randomization.

### Required environment / mediator endpoints

- rainfall event identity and amount;
- direct capitulum wetting or wetness-duration sensor/score;
- internal/head-surface temperature and humidity;
- visible water entry or retained moisture;
- optional UV exposure only if measurement is field-valid.

### Reproductive-process endpoints

- pollen viability after exposure;
- stigma pollen deposition;
- pollen wash-off / loss proxy where validated;
- effective pollinator contact and visit rate;
- florivore/seed-predator visitation as a competing pathway.

### Fitness endpoint

Primary: filled/viable achene proportion and count per experimental capitulum.

Secondary: total achene number and seed mass.

### Primary causal contrast

The decisive prediction is an **orientation × rain/wetting interaction**, not a universal main effect of orientation.

Expected pattern:

- under dry conditions, upward versus downward fitness difference small;
- under high wetting/rain exposure, experimentally upward heads show greater reproductive-tissue damage/process loss and lower filled-achene production than natural/sham downward heads.

### Falsification

The rain-protection mechanism is not supported if manipulation changes orientation but does not change wetting exposure or reproductive-process endpoints, even if seed output differs.

If wetting changes but fitness does not, orientation affects exposure but the proposed protection pathway lacks demonstrated fitness consequence.

## P02 — JPN36 phyllary/access experiment

### Existing readiness

The existing JPN36 protocol already freezes:

- reversible damage-free access proxy versus sham;
- 1:1 within predeclared matched pairs;
- one head per individual;
- target 12 pairs for the pilot;
- deterministic randomization seed;
- no replacement after assignment;
- intention-to-treat loss/failure ledger.

Field execution remains unauthorized until live-state, abundance, permission and manipulation-feasibility gates pass.

### Causal question

> Do appressed/protective phyllaries preserve seed output by restricting reproductive-antagonist access rather than by improving pollinator attraction?

### Required mediator endpoints

- verified mechanical access change caused by manipulation;
- antagonist entry/attack;
- oviposition or larval damage where observable;
- legitimate pollinator access;
- effective pollen transfer;
- wetting as a competing protective mechanism.

### Fitness endpoint

Primary: mature/filled viable achenes.

### Decisive causal pattern

`access opening → antagonist access/damage ↑ → viable seed ↓`

with no necessary increase in legitimate pollinator performance.

### Falsification

If the access proxy does not alter antagonist access, the manipulation is mechanically invalid.

If antagonist access changes but seed output does not, mechanical enemy exclusion is not demonstrated as a fitness mechanism in the tested context.

## P03 — JPN15 stickiness neutralization

### Existing readiness

JPN15 is the primary necessity target because it is naturally sticky and is the ML sister comparator of nonsticky JPN06. The existing programme correctly treats JPN06 adhesive restoration as a secondary sufficiency test only after material-equivalence and non-target-effect validation.

### Causal question

> Does natural stickiness alter reproductive fitness by filtering the local arthropod community, and does its net value depend on antagonist pressure and trait cost?

### Primary design

Within JPN15:

- natural sticky;
- stickiness neutralized;
- sham treatment.

Neutralization must preserve surface geometry and avoid treatment odour/toxicity effects as far as experimentally possible.

### Required mediator endpoints

Record arthropods by functional guild:

- legitimate pollinators;
- florivores;
- seed predators;
- aphids/sap feeders;
- ants;
- predators;
- parasitoids/other natural enemies where resolvable.

Also record direct trap contact / bypass behaviour rather than counting presence alone.

### Process endpoints

- effective pollen transfer;
- antagonist attack/damage;
- guild-specific access rate;
- optional community composition metric declared before outcome opening.

### Fitness endpoint

Primary: mature/filled viable achenes.

### Decisive causal pattern

No universal direction is preregistered for all sites.

The leading model predicts:

`stickiness → arthropod guild composition/access → pollination and/or antagonist pressure → viable seed`

and allows the net fitness sign to depend on local enemy pressure.

### Secondary sufficiency test

JPN06 artificial restoration is only opened after adhesive material equivalence and non-target-effect validation. It is not required for the primary JPN15 necessity test.

## Cross-module synthesis

The strongest aza3 result would not be three unrelated significant treatment effects.

The target synthesis is:

| Capitulum component | Manipulated mediator | Predicted functional interface | Shared final endpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | rain/wetting exposure | abiotic reproductive protection / timing | filled viable achenes |
| Phyllary posture | mechanical head access | reproductive-antagonist exclusion | filled viable achenes |
| Stickiness | arthropod access/community | context-dependent arthropod filtering | filled viable achenes |

Support for different mediator chains would provide a causal explanation for the historical mosaic found by EAzami: different capitulum components can be selected through different ecological interfaces.

## Environment-dependent selection test

Where replication permits, estimate treatment × environment interactions rather than only treatment main effects.

Priority interactions:

- orientation treatment × rain/wetting exposure;
- phyllary treatment × ambient antagonist pressure;
- stickiness treatment × local antagonist/community pressure.

These interactions test whether the fitness value of a component changes with the ecological pressure it is hypothesized to mediate.

## How aza3 returns to evolutionary history

Do **not** begin by screening many new palaeoclimate variables.

First identify the causal mediator experimentally.

Only after a mediator is supported should historical analysis be reopened using the closest reconstructable proxy.

Examples:

- orientation: if wetting duration/rain events mediate fitness, historical follow-up targets precipitation-event structure or the closest defensible palaeoclimate proxy, not generic BIOCLIM fishing;
- phyllary: if enemy access mediates fitness, historical inference requires a defensible proxy for antagonist pressure or habitat context; climate alone is insufficient;
- stickiness: if arthropod-community filtering mediates fitness, historical inference should focus on environmental conditions that predict the relevant guild balance, not stickiness–climate correlation by itself.

## Evidence ladder after aza3

EAzami:
- recurrent component evolution;
- mosaic assembly;
- functional hypotheses;
- orientation transition ecology.

aza3:
- focal trait manipulation;
- mediator;
- process;
- reproductive fitness;
- trait × environment selection heterogeneity.

Only if these links hold should the combined programme use the word **adaptation** for a focal capitulum state.

## Execution priority

1. **Orientation × rain/wetting** — now the highest-information experiment because EAzami supplies the strongest transition-level ecological prediction and the causal mediator remains open.
2. **JPN15 stickiness neutralization** — strongest within-*Cirsium* necessity test and already well specified.
3. **JPN36 phyllary/access pilot** — strong historical localization and clear antagonist-access hypothesis, but field authorization/live feasibility remain gating.
4. JPN06 restoration — secondary sufficiency test only.

## Stop rules

- Do not infer adaptation from EAzami recurrence alone.
- Do not call a seed-output treatment effect mechanistic confirmation without the preregistered mediator moving in the predicted direction.
- Do not rescue a failed mediator by switching to another measured mediator after outcome opening.
- Do not reopen broad historical climate screening before a focal causal mediator is established.
- Do not pool the three traits into one universal ecological mechanism.
