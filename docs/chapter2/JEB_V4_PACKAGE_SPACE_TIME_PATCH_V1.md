### Orientation direction is robust but threshold support is occurrence-source sensitive

Across all six accepted topologies, BIO1 remains negative (D−U = −1.001 to −0.994 SD; P=0.04819–0.04904) and BIO15 remains positive (+1.136 to +1.143 SD; P=0.03789–0.03980); both directions survive 66/66 topology × species-LOO fits.
