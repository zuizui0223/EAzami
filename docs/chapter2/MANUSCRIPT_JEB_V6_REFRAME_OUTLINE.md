## Working title direction

> **Evolutionary depth and scale-dependent environmental assembly of capitulum diversity in an East-Asian thistle radiation**

The exact title remains provisional until the complete EAzami atlas is available.

## Central question

> **Which capitulum traits align with which environments within the focal radiation when EAzami is allowed to discover its own trait–environment structure, how repeatedly and deeply were those traits assembled through evolutionary history, and when do those focal patterns agree with or depart from the global spatial patterns in Azami?**

## Manuscript logic

### Introduction

1. Complex structures can be modular in evolutionary history even when integrated in the present.
2. Present environmental sorting and historical origin are distinct questions.
3. A second distinction is equally important: a focal radiation should not be analysed only through environmental relationships discovered in a global atlas.
4. Therefore the focal radiation needs its own complete exploratory trait × environment baseline before cross-scale concordance is interpreted.
5. Chapter 2 combines that focal ecological atlas with repeated-history, chronology and palaeoenvironmental layers.

### Question 1 — What is the EAzami current trait × environment field?

Run every available continuous endpoint against every frozen current environmental predictor in the common universe. Retain the complete matrix, multiplicity, coverage and sensitivity labels.

The current 7 × 4 pilot is only the first frozen stage:

- n = 8 taxa after the environment-depth gate;
- 28 tests;
- 0 BH-supported rows;
- strongest raw lead shape width CV × BIO12: rho=+0.857, exact P=0.0107, q=0.30;
- lightness × BIO4 raw boundary lead: rho=-0.723, P=0.0500, q=0.5375;
- orientation × BIO12 approximately zero in this small continuous panel: rho=+0.0238, P=0.9768;
- orientation × BIO4 larger but uncorrected: rho=+0.714, P=0.0576.

These pilot results are not manuscript candidates yet. Their role is to demonstrate that EAzami discovery cannot be replaced by Azami-derived predictor selection.

### Question 2 — Which current EAzami relationships survive phylogenetic/topology uncertainty?

Apply the focal nuclear-history uncertainty to the complete exploratory atlas rather than only orientation.

Report:

- effect/sign envelope across admitted topologies;
- leave-one-taxon-out stability;
- coverage/not_evaluable;
- multiplicity status from the complete EAzami family.

### Question 3 — How deep and recurrent are the traits?

Discrete history remains independent of current ecology:

- orientation: 4–6 minimum changes;
- phyllary posture: 3;
- involucre stickiness: 5;
- 0/3 discrete trait pairs pass the robust shared-transition-localization rule.

Continuous trait history is analysed only where morph/sample homology and coverage support it.

### Question 4 — What historical environmental trajectories accompanied dated events?

Use dated events and commensurate palaeoenvironmental variables only where independently recoverable.

The existing orientation chronology × palaeolocation envelope remains one valid event-specific example:

- 94 age pairs × 4 regions = 376 scenarios;
- historical direction unresolved.

It is no longer presented as the central confirmation test of the Azami BIO12 result.

Additional event/environment analyses should enter because the EAzami history and public data make them evaluable, not because Azami highlighted the same predictor.

### Question 5 — Where do Azami and EAzami agree?

Only after Questions 1–4 are frozen, compare the two chapters.

Cross-chapter classes:

- concordant;
- discordant;
- EAzami-only;
- Azami-only;
- not comparable.

The comparison unit can be an exact endpoint × predictor pair when homologous, or a broader environmental block when variables differ but the domain is genuinely commensurate.

### Question 6 — What does this imply for whole-capitulum assembly?

Integrate:

- complete EAzami current environmental field;
- unequal discrete evolutionary depth;
- shared-history failures;
- repeated coarse remodelling in the two white-lineage systems;
- cross-chapter concordance and discordance.

The whole-capitulum conclusion must be derived after the complete field is shown. Do not make `modular hierarchical selection mosaic` the premise that determines which environmental rows are examined.
