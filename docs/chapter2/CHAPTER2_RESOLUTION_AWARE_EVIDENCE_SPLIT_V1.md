## The result language

Chapter 2 does not claim recurrent evolution. It estimates topology-conditioned **minimum state-change counts**. The phrase `at least` is the central scientific device because it remains valid when directions, ancestral assignments and individual event placements are unresolved.

The active time-axis conclusion is:

> **Within the admitted public tree ensemble, orientation requires at least four state changes, phyllary posture at least three and stickiness at least five. These are lower bounds on state changes, not counts of independent origins, repeated gains, convergence or adaptation.**

## 1. What is known with high confidence

1. Published phylogenomics places 36 of 38 sampled Japanese concepts in the dominant radiation.
2. The authority-covered dominant-radiation subset contains at least three harmonized orientation x stickiness configurations.
3. Every admitted topology requires at least four orientation changes, at least three phyllary-posture changes and at least five stickiness changes.
4. Minimum-count stability does not guarantee equal event-depth or placement resolution. Median bootstrap depth envelopes are orientation 0.795–0.994, phyllary 0.695–1.000 and stickiness 0.937–0.954. Current run-329 JPN36 fractions are 22.7% for orientation and 72.8% for phyllary, whereas JPN06 stickiness is 99.5% after the frozen JPN24 extension.
5. Species-tip coding loses extant state information in four of four audited polymorphic systems; the only morph-linked testable system changes from one to two minimum changes.

Items 1-3 are the positive Chapter 2 biological result. Items 4-5 define the resolution boundary and the next sampling problem.

## 2. What must not be reported as a negative biological conclusion

The following results are analysis decisions but do not establish biological absence:

- zero of three trait pairs meets the cross-treatment localization rule;
- zero of four direct continuous traits passes the corrected seven-taxon rule;
- the primary BIO15 orientation-niche result is borderline and branchwise tests are unsupported;
- the reconstruction-aware continuous test returns P=0.3504 and the fixed JPN29-excluded sensitivity P=0.1959;
- broad continuous Japan38 history is identity- and coverage-limited.

These analyses cannot tell whether traits truly lack a shared history, phylogenetic retention or environmental association. Their correct result class is **not identifiable at current resolution**, not biological negative.

## 3. Narrow hypotheses that are genuinely contradicted

Some negative conclusions are legitimate because the hypothesis is deterministic or the model family was frozen:

- one colonization-history class does not deterministically assign one observed capitulum configuration;
- sparse cytotype records contradict a strict one-ploidy-to-one-orientation rule, without proving statistical independence;
- five strict factorial programmes contradict universal pollinator dominance and universal antagonist dominance;
- the frozen v3.1 generator set has zero absolutely adequate families;
- frozen `NULL_COUPLED` reproduces the held-out primary support pattern in 0/64 draws;
- the post-heldout among-only candidate set has zero adequate families.

Simulation negatives apply to the declared generators and targets only. Simulated acceptance frequencies are not ecological prevalence, likelihood, posterior probability or evidence that one biological mechanism is true.

## 4. Meta-analysis disposition

The meta-analysis programme produces strong Chapter 3 material but does not explain the Chapter 2 time-axis counts:

- reproductive-herbivory meta-analysis: RR=2.674 across four study clusters, a general antagonist seed-output cost rather than a protective-trait effect;
- multi-agent synthesis: antagonist 2, mixed 2 and pollinator 1 among five strict programmes, supporting a selection-mosaic design rather than focal agent dominance;
- demographic synthesis: fecundity effects transmit consistently in four studies, context-dependently in one and are blocked in one;
- pollinator assurance: dependence is common but not equivalent to open pollen limitation, and no pooled effect is authorized;
- orientation fitness: k=2 and I2=93.6%, so it is a feasibility prior only;
- broad functional calibration: endpoint and sham selection only; no pooled cross-angiosperm effect is authorized.

The generic meta-analysis ceiling has been reached for the current six HGA hypotheses. The next information gain comes from linked focal observations, not another heterogeneous literature accumulation.

1. JPN06-JPN15 population-aware nuclear/network sampling plus same-individual stickiness to test the current JPN06 99.5% terminal-placement concentration and 100/100 sister contrast;

Present-state covariance simulations remain with Chapter 1. Generic functional meta-analysis enters Chapter 3 only as a bounded prior and design constraint. Field-readiness protocols enter Chapter 3 only with `field_execution_authorized=false` until rights and conservation gates pass.

## Claim ceiling

Chapter 2 establishes observed configuration diversity, topology-conditioned minimum-change lower bounds and unequal topology-only relative lineage-depth and event-placement resolution. Exactly three authority-backed discrete histories are complete. It does not establish independent origins, repeated gains, convergence, a shared or independent developmental module, adaptation, absolute timing or causal ecological drivers.
