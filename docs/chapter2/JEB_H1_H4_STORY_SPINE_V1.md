# JEB Chapter 2 story spine after H1–H4

**Status:** 2026-09-04

## Core paper question

> **How was a multidimensional capitulum assembled through a young radiation, and does the ecological regime associated with one repeatedly changing component today also describe the environment in which that component originated?**

This gives the paper two linked but non-identical biological questions:

1. **assembly architecture:** how did the component traits accumulate through the radiation?
2. **ecological persistence:** does present trait–niche tracking persist backward to trait origin?

## Main answer

> **The capitulum was assembled mosaically at unequal evolutionary depths. Orientation additionally shows bidirectional present-niche regime tracking, but that same regime is not supported for the only bounded historical U→D origin event.**

The paper is therefore not “mosaic assembly plus a failed climate search.” It is:

```text
mosaic assembly through time
        ↓
orientation as an ecological bridge
        ↓
bidirectional present-niche tracking
        ↓
explicit historical-persistence falsification
        ↓
present tracking ≠ identified origin regime
```

# Proposed result order

## Result 1 — Diversity is assembled within one young radiation

- 36/38 Japanese concepts in the dominant radiation.
- multiple capitulum configurations remain within it.

**Answer:** large configuration diversity is an intra-radiation problem.

## Result 2 — Three components repeatedly change but at unequal depth

- orientation 4–6 minimum changes;
- phyllary 3;
- stickiness 5;
- paired depth ordering 1000/1000, 993/1000, 905/1000;
- coverage-matched median ordering 97.5% / 96.5%;
- strict tails overlap.

**Answer:** recurrence alone misses the temporal geometry of component assembly.

## Result 3 — Component changes are not synchronized

- 0/3 trait pairs pass robust shared localization.

**Answer:** simplest whole-capitulum synchronized history unsupported → mosaic historical assembly.

## Result 4 — Present orientation ecology is scale-partitioned

- BIO12 among-only;
- BIO1 within-only plus negative East-Asian state contrast;
- BIO15 strong East-Asian contrast but no matching positive within response.

**Answer:** present ecological association depends on scale.

## Result 5 — Fixed transition-regime H1 is supported

Predeclared vector:

`U→D = BIO15 up + BIO1 down`

- 12-taxon exact rank 16/792 = 2.02%;
- 13-taxon sensitivity 19/1716 = 1.11%;
- strict 9-taxon rank 4/126 = 3.17%;
- Japan-only 10/56 = 17.86% → regional boundary.

**Answer:** repeated orientation transitions are associated with a composite East-Asian present niche regime, not a uniquely identified BIO15 driver.

## Result 6 — H2/H3 show bidirectional and distributed tracking

- U→D forward median 0.320891;
- D→U reverse median 0.339529;
- both positive 6/6 topologies;
- exact bidirectional floor 3/126 = 2.38%;
- delete each taxon once: both directions remain positive in 9/9 deletion panels × 6/6 topologies;
- exact <=0.05 survives 3/9 deletions, so exceptionality is multi-taxon-sensitive.

**Answer:** the signal is not only a static D-vs-U contrast or one-lineage artifact; under the declared estimator the two orientation states track opposite sides of the same present niche regime.

## Result 7 — H4 rejects persistence of the present regime at the dated origin event

Historical H4 signs for the core-*Nipponocirsium* U→D event:

`BIO15 delta > 0 AND BIO1 delta < 0`

- overall match 99/376 = 26.3%;
- Taiwan 21.3%;
- Ryukyu 9.6%;
- southern Japan 43.6%;
- East-Asia core 30.9%;
- only 6/94 chronologies match in 4/4 regions;
- central 0.79→0.74 Ma: BIO1 decreases but BIO15 also decreases in 4/4 regions.

**Answer:** current reversible trait–niche tracking should not be projected backward as the environmental regime of origin.

## Result 8 — Broader historical-cause ceiling remains

- 0/324 robust 17-BIOCLIM event-level classes;
- 0/21 robust sea-level classes.

**Answer:** origin-time causal mechanism remains less identifiable than assembly and present tracking.

# Discussion spine

## Discussion 1 — Complex phenotype assembly is temporally mosaic

Main result remains multi-trait and general.

## Discussion 2 — Ecology can track an evolved state without explaining its origin

Use H1–H4 sequence:

- state association alone could be lineage placement;
- H1 moves to branchwise transition-regime concordance;
- H2 shows the relation in both directions;
- H3 rules out dependence on one strict-panel taxon;
- H4 rejects simple persistence of that regime at the sole bounded origin event.

This is the most important conceptual advance beyond V7.

## Discussion 3 — Origin and maintenance/current sorting are different estimands

Do not say that maintenance is experimentally proven. The supported statement is:

> **The environmental regime organizing extant lineage/state correspondence need not be the regime under which a trait originated.**

Current data are compatible with later ecological sorting, changing selective regimes, exaptation, demographic filtering, or other lineage-linked processes.

## Discussion 4 — Why the historical cause remains unresolved

H4 specifically rejects one projection; 0/324 and 0/21 bound broader coarse explanations. New causal resolution requires mediator/fitness or stronger event-linked ancestry data, not more correlated coarse climate screening.

# Final conclusion wording target

> **A young East-Asian *Cirsium* radiation assembled capitulum diversity mosaically, with component traits recurring at unequal evolutionary depths rather than through one synchronized history. Orientation provides a deeper ecological bridge: inferred U→D and D→U changes track opposite sides of a fixed present-day two-axis niche regime, and this directional relation survives deletion of any single strict-panel taxon. Yet the same regime matches only 26.3% of the chronology × palaeolocation scenarios for the sole calendarized U→D event and fails the predeclared historical-persistence criterion. Present ecological organization therefore cannot be assumed to reveal the environment of phenotypic origin. Assembly history and present niche tracking are substantially more identifiable than the historical causal regime that produced the trait.**

# Submission positioning

The title **Repeated mosaic assembly at unequal evolutionary depths in a young thistle radiation** remains valid because the multi-trait assembly result is still the paper-wide headline.

The orientation H1–H4 sequence should be presented as the paper’s second conceptual contribution:

> **an explicit demonstration that present trait–niche tracking can be transition-linked and bidirectional yet still fail as a model of the historical origin environment.**

This is stronger and more surprising than simply saying that “historical cause is unresolved.”
