## Chapter 2 position

Chapter 1 measures capitulum phenotype across present space. Chapter 2 asks:

> **How many state changes are minimally required in the traits that form alternative capitulum configurations, at what relative lineage depth can those histories occur, and how far can existing data explain or constrain the observed states?**

The operational sequence is:

**minimum state-change lower bound → relative lineage-depth / named-edge event resolution → ecological explanatory reach → explicit Chapter 3 boundary**.

The paper does not require every plausible driver to be modelled. It reports what current joined data can resolve and stops when data overlap is insufficient.

## Four contributions

### 1. Configuration diversity with multiple minimum changes

36 of 38 sampled Japanese concepts occur in the dominant radiation. The authority-covered subset contains at least three harmonized orientation × stickiness configurations.

The accepted topology ensemble requires multiple minimum changes in all three completed discrete trait histories:

- orientation: 20 resolved concepts; ML=6; UFBoot=4–6, median 5;
- phyllary posture: 10 resolved concepts; exactly 3 changes in all 1,000 UFBoot trees;
- stickiness: 13 resolved concepts; exactly 5 changes in all 1,000 UFBoot trees.

These are topology-conditioned lower bounds, not counts of independent origins, convergence or evolutionary rates.

### 2. Minimum count, relative depth and named-edge resolution are different properties

Median bootstrap relative lineage-depth envelopes are:

- orientation: 0.795–0.994;
- phyllary posture: 0.695–1.000;
- stickiness: 0.937–0.954.

Current forced-edge fractions include orientation JPN36=0.227, phyllary JPN36=0.728, stickiness JPN06=0.995 and JPN36=0.707. Stickiness is count-stable and depth-constrained, phyllary posture is count-stable but depth-ambiguous, and orientation remains weakly localized.

The three-coordinate **event resolution** result separates **minimum-count stability**, relative lineage-depth and named-edge localization. Zero of three trait pairs meets the cross-treatment shared-localization rule. This constrains simple whole-capitulum common lability without proving independent modules.

### 3. Ecological explanatory reach is asymmetric

The frozen East-Asian orientation panel contains nine taxa, U=5 and D=4, each with at least ten independent thinned environment-complete occurrences.

Across six AU-nonrejected optimized topologies:

- BIO15 D−U = +1.320 to +1.330 SD; P=0.05054–0.05239;
- BIO1 D−U = −0.975 to −0.967 SD; P=0.09604–0.09793;
- accepted-topology sign agreement = 6/6 for both;
- species LOO sign agreement = 54/54 for both.

The branchwise diagnostic has the same directions on all six topologies, but its permutation thresholds are not crossed. The correct classification is therefore **orientation=`unresolved`**: a strong and robust directional correspondence exists, but the frozen inferential gate is not crossed.

Phyllary posture and stickiness are **`not_evaluable`** at the same climate gate because each overlaps only two resolved n≥10 taxa and neither overlap contains two state classes. This is a data-resolution result, not evidence of no ecological relationship.

Held-out mean-null / phylogeny-only comparisons remain reproducible Supporting Information diagnostics. They are **not required** for the main ecological-reach classification because the biological question is correspondence/evaluability and the current panel has only nine taxa.

### 4. Existing non-climate evidence already constrains simple alternatives

Current data do more than climate alone, but not all factors can be promoted to fitted trait-explanation models.

- **Cytotype/ploidy:** nine concepts have source-backed cytotype data. Upward/ascending states occur at 2x, 4x and 6x, and diploids include both upward and downward states. A deterministic one-to-one ploidy explanation of orientation is inconsistent with the observed panel.
- **Broad biogeographic history:** multiple capitulum configurations occur within the same dominant radiation, so configuration does not map one-to-one onto the broad colonization-history class.
- **Population/genetic structure:** independent Japanese MIG-seq/RAD work and a reusable 2022 *C. maritimum* MIG-seq dataset show that biologically relevant structure can occur below a species tip. These data constrain the assumption that one named species is one homogeneous genomic unit.
- **Independent nuclear topology evidence:** pre-2025 Korean rDNA, Cardueae Hyb-Seq, a 2024 *C. nipponicum* nuclear genome, and 2025/2026 local phylotranscriptomics show that the accepted Comp1061 tree is the full-panel **harmonized common-locus scaffold**, not the sole nuclear evidence.
- **Pollinator/enemy context:** genus-level evidence is biologically strong but is not yet joined densely enough to Japan38 trait states for a comparable phylogeny-aware explanatory analysis.

These are explicit constraints on simple alternatives. They do not identify the selective cause of any reconstructed transition.

## Nuclear evidence boundary

The accepted Japan38 Comp1061 reconstruction remains the historical scaffold because it is a homologous common-locus framework spanning the complete admitted panel. It must not be described as the only nuclear evidence.

The independent audit now retains, among others:

- 2012/2015 Korean nrDNA studies;
- Japanese KAKEN `17K07524` MIG-seq/RAD work;
- public 2022 *C. maritimum* MIG-seq Genepop data;
- 2018/2020 *C. japonicum* transcriptome resources;
- the 2024 *C. nipponicum* nuclear reference genome;
- 2019 Cardueae Compositae1061 Hyb-Seq;
- 2025/2026 East-Asian phylotranscriptomic species-tree/network studies.

These datasets remain separated by estimand and marker system. Heterogeneous rDNA, MIG-seq, transcriptomic and reference-genome branch lengths are not combined into a synthetic tree.

## Claim ceiling

The paper establishes topology-conditioned minimum-change lower bounds, observed configuration diversity, trait-dependent relative lineage-depth and named-edge resolution, and bounded present ecological explanatory reach. It also constrains deterministic ploidy, broad-history and species-tip assumptions using existing data. Relative lineage-depth is not absolute time. Present trait–climate correspondence is not historical niche reconstruction, event-specific environmental matching, convergence, adaptation or causal selection. `not_evaluable` is a data-resolution result, not a biological negative.
