# Doctoral meta-analysis resolution ceiling — 2026-08-22

## Decision

The interaction/meta-analysis programme has reached its current **decision ceiling** for all six HGA hypotheses.

This is a stop rule for generic literature accumulation, not a claim that the focal *Cirsium* biology is solved.

Further generic meta-analysis is allowed only when a prespecified reopening trigger in `data/evidence/doctoral_meta_resolution_gate_v1.csv` is met.

## What the meta-analysis has resolved

### HGA0 — direct broad-abiotic scaling — weakened

The simple model that productivity, fertility, elevation, or another broad abiotic gradient directly and monotonically scales the fitness consequence of floral interactions is not supported by the current *Cirsium* demographic literature. Across five broad-context comparisons, no general monotonic moderator emerged, whereas interaction-inclusive structural models consistently outperformed environment-only models.

Abiotic environment remains mechanistically important at the head scale (temperature, radiation, rain/wetting), but broad climate is no longer treated as the main causal answer.

### HGA1 — demographic gate / bottleneck switching — working support

Reproductive-antagonist effects can propagate from fecundity to recruitment/population growth, become context dependent, or disappear at downstream bottlenecks. Safe sites, disturbance, and density dependence are more consistent gates than broad climate gradients.

Meta-analysis can establish this architecture but cannot classify the focal P001–P014 populations as seed-limited versus safe-site-limited. That requires the conditional recruitment pilot after a focal seed-output contrast exists.

### HGA2 — reproductive-assurance buffering — working support

Animal-pollinator dependence and current open pollen limitation are different quantities. Autonomous selfing, donor spacing, flowering overlap, and local density can buffer or expose pollination limitation.

The literature is sufficient to reject visits as a direct fitness proxy. Focal assurance still requires bagged/open/supplemented seed-fitness measurements.

### HGA3 — assurance-dependent selection mosaic — general working meta-support

Two independent meta layers now converge:

1. restricted experimental pollinator-gradient reanalysis: broad trait class does not robustly identify `|Δβpoll|`, and experimentally increasing pollen limitation does not strengthen all traits uniformly;
2. strict multi-agent manipulation synthesis: among five independent programmes, two are antagonist-dominant, one pollinator-dominant, and two mixed/no-fixed-dominance; at least two categories remain after leaving out any one programme.

Therefore a universal pollinator-dominance or antagonist-dominance model is rejected. Selection mosaics can arise from nonadditive interactions or from additive opposing gradients whose relative intensities change among populations/times.

The focal *Cirsium* agent balance is **not** identified by this result. The decisive test remains same-population pollination supplementation × post-anthesis antagonist protection, measured on one seed-fitness scale, while estimating local trait-to-interaction leverage.

### HGA4 — interaction partitioning / trade-off mitigation — mechanistic candidate at meta ceiling

The general trade-off-mitigation concept is already established in the floral literature. Multiple strategies are documented or suggested: temporal switching, schedule optimization, active exclusion, and combinational trait solutions.

Evidence relevant to EAzami includes:

- *Cirsium arvense* diel scent emission aligns with pollinator activity and is low when florivores are active;
- external orientation experiments show that timing/efficiency or abiotic protection can change reproductive performance even when simple total-visitation interpretations fail;
- newer orientation work directly shows combined pollination-efficiency and rain/solar-protection pathways;
- a demographic ant–plant experiment provides an important counterexample: temporal partitioning can coexist with residual pollination costs.

The missing literature class is narrow: replicated manipulations that change a putative partitioning trait and simultaneously close **mutualist channel + antagonist channel + final reproductive fitness** on a harmonizable scale. Until such a set reaches at least five independent experiments, a generic pooled partitioning effect is not justified.

For the thesis, HGA4 therefore moves to focal experimental testing rather than further generic literature accumulation.

### HGA5 — multi-stage modularity — unresolved and beyond the current meta ceiling

Meta-analysis cannot decide modular evolvability versus common lability. Supporting evidence exists for weak cross-module coupling and module-specific mechanism maps, but the robust structural screen still allows a common-lability full model.

The missing information is not another paper on one trait. It is joint, ancestry-controlled measurement of multiple homologous capitulum modules, interactions, and fitness in the same focal radiation.

The decisive comparison is:

1. one shared latent/common-lability response structure;
2. module-specific responses and covariance;
3. module-specific `trait -> effective interaction/protection -> seed fitness` paths;
4. out-of-sample comparison that penalizes extra parameters.

## New causal architecture after meta-analysis

The current best working architecture is:

`local abiotic + population context`

→ `interaction opportunity / reproductive assurance / enemy pressure`

× `local trait-to-interaction functional leverage`

→ `effective pollination and antagonist damage`

→ `seed fitness`

→ `safe-site / disturbance / density gate`

→ `recruitment / population consequence`.

Different capitulum modules may enter at different arrows, but that last statement remains HGA5 rather than a conclusion.

## Mainline action after this gate

Stop generic interaction meta-analysis unless a reopening trigger appears.

Proceed with focal Aim 2 discrimination:

1. orientation first — preserve time windows, head temperature, wetting/pollen viability, effective contacts, antagonist exposure, final achenes;
2. W/coloured comparison only where flowering overlaps — preserve local colour availability and effective contacts rather than visits alone;
3. nested subset: pollination supplementation × post-anthesis antagonist protection on the same seed-fitness scale;
4. estimate local leverage: trait→effective-contact and trait→damage relationships;
5. only after a clear seed-output contrast, run the seed-addition × microsite recruitment gate pilot;
6. use ancestry-controlled multivariate data to compare common-lability versus module-specific causal models.

No new Aim, broad population expansion, or generic literature search is authorized by this gate.
