# Draft data request — Chang et al. 2026 machine-readable phylogenetic trees

Status: **draft only; not sent**  
Corresponding author listed in the final article: Yen-Hsueh Tseng (`tsengyh2014@tfri.gov.tw`)

Suggested subject:

`Request for machine-readable phylogenetic tree files from Chang et al. (2026) BMC Plant Biology`

Suggested message:

Dear Dr. Tseng,

I am working on a comparative evolutionary analysis of flower-colour transitions in East Asian *Cirsium* and have been using your 2026 BMC Plant Biology paper, “Phylotranscriptomics and genome-size evidence clarify the Taiwanese *Cirsium japonicum* complex and delimit *C. brevicaule* and allied East Asian thistles” (doi: 10.1186/s12870-026-08097-6), as an important phylogenetic reference.

The raw RNA-seq data under BioProject PRJNA1311153 and the published supplementary material are very helpful. For ancestral-state and topology-sensitivity analyses, I would also like to use the exact machine-readable phylogenetic outputs rather than digitizing the published figures.

If available, would you be willing to share any of the following files?

1. the all-sample ASTRAL species tree shown in Fig. 1A (Newick preferred);
2. the Cirsium ASTRAL tree used for SODA/PTP in Fig. 1E;
3. the 52-orthogroup Bayesian MCC tree used for the Cirsium-only species-delimitation/DensiTree analysis in Fig. 1D;
4. the posterior `.trees` sample underlying the Fig. 1D DensiTree, if convenient to share;
5. the 50-orthogroup time-calibrated MCC tree shown in Fig. 2, ideally retaining node heights, posterior support and HPD annotations;
6. if available, the posterior `.trees`, BEAST XML and log files for the 50-orthogroup dated analysis;
7. the RAxML gene-tree set supplied to ASTRAL and/or a list of the retained orthogroups; and
8. any taxon-label/voucher mapping needed to interpret the tree tip labels.

My main purpose is to test whether the white-flowered *C. brevicaule* / bluish-purple *C. irumtiense* contrast is most consistent with white-flower loss, coloured-flower regain, ancestral polymorphism, or reticulate history while propagating the phylogenetic uncertainty in your analysis. I would cite the paper and acknowledge the source of any shared files; I would also be happy to follow any conditions you prefer for reuse or attribution.

If only the final ASTRAL and dated MCC Newick/Nexus trees are readily available, those two files alone would already be extremely useful.

Thank you very much for your time and for making the raw sequence data publicly available.

Best regards,
Rachel Zhang / Ruiqi Zhang
