# EAzami — rapid East Asian/Japanese *Cirsium* radiation

EAzami is organized around one doctoral-level question:

> **How did one young East-Asian/Japanese *Cirsium* radiation generate such large capitulum diversity so quickly, and which ecological selection pressures repeatedly favour different capitulum trait modules?**

## Azami -> EAzami zoom

`zuizui0223/azami` and EAzami answer different questions.

### Azami = global observational discovery

Azami stops at:

`public images -> continuous capitulum phenotypes -> within-taxon distributions -> within/among-taxon environmental structure`

It establishes that visible capitulum variation is substantial below taxon means and that orientation and visible colour have particularly clear environmental structure. Its involucre/spine measurements are 2-D image proxies. Azami does **not** claim pollinator causation, defence, local adaptation, evolutionary rate, parallel evolution or adaptive radiation.

### EAzami = multi-trait adaptive-evolution zoom

EAzami begins with the patterns frozen by Azami and asks, for every capitulum module:

`selection pressure`

`-> ecological mechanism`

`-> evolutionary history / repeated transitions`

`-> reproductive fitness`

The modules are treated in parallel:

`display quantity | orientation | phyllary/spine defence | stickiness/mucilage | flower colour/pigmentation | validated architecture`

Orientation is the **first experimentally tractable module**, not the central doctoral trait.

## Central hypotheses

### Selection mosaic

Different combinations of pollinator opportunity, reproductive assurance, reproductive-antagonist pressure, rain/wetting, UV, temperature, drought, density and resource context can favour different capitulum modules or trait combinations. There is no universal best capitulum.

### Modular evolvability

Standing ancestral variation, gene flow/introgression and cytotype/genome changes may allow semi-independent developmental/genetic modules for display, orientation, defence and pigmentation to be retained and redeployed across shallow lineages, so capitulum phenotype can diverge faster than genome-wide lineage sorting.

### Competing common-lability model

One shared whole-capitulum lability axis may generate correlated phenotype variation without semi-independent ecological modules. Modular evolvability is supported only if module-specific transition histories and ecological-fitness paths outperform this shared-latent alternative.

Neither evolutionary model is a recovered conclusion yet.

## What the meta-analysis now establishes

The generic interaction/meta-analysis phase has reached its current decision ceiling.

- **single universal driver:** direct climate-only or pollinator-only explanations are insufficient as general models;
- **selection mosaic:** five strict pollination x antagonism manipulation programmes give antagonist 2 / pollinator 1 / mixed 2; no universal dominant agent survives as a general model;
- **reproductive antagonist pressure:** 4 independent data-generation studies / 9 contrasts; reduced-herbivory seed-output RR = **2.674** (95% CI **2.388-2.993**), equivalent to **62.6%** ambient loss of potential seed output;
- **reproductive assurance:** animal-pollinator dependence is often high, but dependence is not equivalent to current open pollen limitation;
- **demographic gate:** a fecundity effect can transmit, become context-dependent or disappear at the seed-to-recruitment bottleneck;
- **display:** *C. purpuratum* supports simultaneous pollinator benefit and predispersal seed-predator cost, with density/context modifying the pollinator response;
- **orientation:** timing and rain/UV/wetting protection are candidate mechanisms, not a proven *Cirsium* mechanism;
- **phyllary/spine:** defence remains a candidate until direct focal botanical traits are validated;
- **stickiness:** a direct *C. discolor* neutralization experiment weakens generic `sticky inflorescence = seed-predator defence`;
- **colour/pigmentation:** pollinator and abiotic roles are both plausible; `colder -> darker/more pigmented` is retained as a testable hypothesis rather than assumed as a *Cirsium* rule.

Further heterogeneous generic literature accumulation is stopped unless a prespecified reopening trigger in `data/evidence/doctoral_meta_resolution_gate_v1.json` is met.

## East Asian evolutionary-history question

Published phylogenomics places the principal Japanese radiation after a Pleistocene dispersal from continental Asia, around 2.4 Ma. The working system premise is that **36/38 sampled Japanese paper taxon concepts** fall in one dominant young radiation; *C. lineare* is the strongest replicated secondary-history exception and *C. dipsacolepis* remains a secondary-arrival candidate.

EAzami now has an accepted 153-locus empirical compatibility tree with explicit six-topology uncertainty. Orientation is currently the first module with a topology-robust repeated-state result: the conservative 17-taxon panel (U=9, D=8) requires at least five orientation-state changes under all six AU-nonrejected topologies. Direction, ancestral orientation and adaptive cause remain unresolved.

That result is a **proof of concept for the multi-trait programme**, not the final scope. The Japan-wide evolutionary layer must now map display, pigmentation, direct phyllary/spine traits and stickiness/mucilage wherever defensible, using the same voucher-linked nuclear backbone rather than building a separate tree for every module.

## Capitulum adaptive modules

1. **display quantity — capitulum size / head number / local display density**  
   Pollinator discovery and probing versus reproductive-antagonist apparency and resource allocation. Primary prediction: a context-dependent fitness optimum, not `bigger is better`.

2. **orientation — continuous angle / erect / true nodding**  
   Rain/wetting, UV, time-window temperature and effective pollination, with antagonist exposure retained separately. Current repeated-state evidence is strong; adaptive cause is not yet known.

3. **phyllary/spine defensive architecture**  
   Antagonist exclusion versus possible pollinator handling/access cost. Requires direct botanical validation before adaptive inference.

4. **stickiness / mucilage**  
   Kept separate from spines. Generic reproductive-defence evidence is currently weakened; reopen only with a specific Japan-wide or focal signal.

5. **flower colour / pigmentation**  
   Pollinator sensory environment plus cold/UV/drought/oxidative-stress hypotheses. Repeated W/coloured history and molecular re-expression remain unresolved. Direction of any climate-pigmentation relation must be estimated, not assumed.

6. **gross capitulum/involucre architecture**  
   Exploratory structural module. Promote only direct repeatable variables with a plausible mechanism and a discriminating fitness prediction.

## Three doctoral Aims

### Aim 1 — Multi-trait evolutionary history and source of repeated variation

Determine how display, orientation, pigmentation, defence architecture and other validated capitulum modules vary across the Japanese radiation, how often they change, and whether repeated states derive from lineage-specific change, standing ancestral variation or introgression, with plastid and cytotype evidence where relevant.

Required chain:

`voucher-linked multi-trait phenotype -> nuclear history -> population ancestry where needed -> plastid -> cytotype -> repeated transition history`

The Japan-wide target-capture breadth panel is therefore **multi-trait**, not orientation-optimized. Every new backbone plant should record, where biologically available:

`capitulum size -> head number/display -> actual orientation -> calibrated visible/UV colour -> direct phyllary/spine architecture -> stickiness -> DNA -> plastid -> cytotype`.

### Aim 2 — Adaptive function and fitness of capitulum modules

For each module, use the same causal template:

`trait -> ecological mechanism -> reproductive fitness`.

Current experimental order is logistical, not conceptual:

1. orientation sham/reorientation first because it gives the cleanest manipulation;
2. W/coloured ecological function where flowering overlap permits;
3. display/density benefit-cost effects nested in the same populations;
4. phyllary/spine only after direct trait validation;
5. stickiness only if a focal signal reopens it.

Orientation has an explicit preregistered causal design (`ORI0-ORI5`) in `docs/AIM2_ORIENTATION_CAUSAL_PREREGISTRATION_V1_2026-08-22.md`.

### Aim 3 — Flower-colour reversibility as a mechanistic flagship

Test whether repeated W<->coloured transitions reuse retained anthocyanin machinery through regulatory/expression changes rather than repeated pathway destruction/rebuilding.

The current exact colour panel is C=17/W=3. The tree gate is ready, but fixed-white breadth and rate identifiability remain load-bearing. Therefore `colour re-expression in C. irumtiense` remains a live hypothesis, not a conclusion.

Required chain for at least two independent W/C transitions:

`ancestry -> coding/regulatory haplotype -> floral RNA -> pigment -> calibrated colour`.

## Adaptive-radiation boundary

Use **rapid radiation** as a system premise.

Do **not** call the radiation adaptive because several capitulum traits differ among taxa. Adaptive-radiation inference requires ancestry-resolved repeated evolution plus trait-specific ecological mechanism and reproductive-fitness evidence, preferably for more than one module.

## Multiscale bridge: within and among species

The programme deliberately keeps both scales.

- **within species / among populations:** environment, pollinator opportunity, enemy pressure, reproductive assurance, ancestry and continuous trait variation;
- **among species / lineages:** independent trait transitions, correlated evolution and repeated ecological contexts on the nuclear history.

The working prediction is that population-level selection mosaics and ancestry-linked variation can feed repeated among-lineage trait states during rapid radiation. Within-species variation is not automatically heritable adaptation or ancestral polymorphism; ancestry and fitness links are required.

## Intended paper architecture

### Paper A — Azami global phenomics

Global continuous within/among-taxon capitulum phenotype and environmental structure. No adaptive claim.

### Paper B — Multi-trait evolutionary history of Japanese capitula

Japan-wide common-locus nuclear history plus voucher/population-validated display, orientation, pigmentation, direct defence architecture and other defensible traits. Main question: did a young radiation repeatedly redeploy multiple capitulum modules, and do their transition contexts differ?

This paper should **not** be reduced to orientation alone.

### Papers C/D — Causal adaptive-function papers

Use the strongest systems emerging from Paper B. Orientation can be first because it is experimentally tractable; colour/re-expression is a likely mechanistic flagship; display and defence become standalone causal papers only if their trait -> mechanism -> fitness paths are strong enough.

### Thesis synthesis

Compare module-specific versus common-lability models and ask whether multiple trait modules jointly support an adaptive-radiation interpretation.

## Preliminary-analysis stop rule

New preliminary work is allowed only when it changes one of three decisions:
1. which population/system to sample;
2. which competing ecological mechanism can be discriminated;
3. which evolutionary/adaptive claim boundary can be advanced.

Do not restart broad climate-only screens or generic heterogeneous interaction pooling. New macroevolutionary analyses should use the Japan-wide ancestry framework to test trait-specific adaptive hypotheses.
